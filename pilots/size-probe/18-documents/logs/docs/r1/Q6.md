# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household's membership expired on Saturday 20 December 2025. Neither named adult declares income support and the household has no unpaid charges. One of the adults comes to the desk on Saturday 10 January 2026 and the desk accepts their renewal payment that day. How much do they pay, and from what date does the renewed membership run?

## Answer

They pay **£30**, and the renewed membership runs **from 10 January 2026**. It lasts 12 calendar months, so the last day is 9 January 2027.

- **Fee:** The original standard fee was £24 (`lend-fees.md` §2.1). The committee replaced it with £30 for any payment accepted on or after 1 January 2026 (`lend-changes.md` §2). The date that counts is the day the desk accepts payment, which here is 10 January 2026. The £12 reduced fee doesn't apply because neither adult declares income support.
- **Start date:** Renewal starts on the date the payment is accepted, not on the old expiry date (`lend-membership.md` §2.1). The lapse from 20 December to 10 January isn't backdated or credited.
- **Renewal block:** The household has no unpaid charges, so nothing stops the renewal (`lend-membership.md` §2.2).

I took "12 calendar months" to end the day before the anniversary. The rules don't state the final day explicitly.
