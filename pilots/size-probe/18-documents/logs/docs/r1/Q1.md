## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: A family of two adults and two children, all living at the same address, plan to take out a household membership for the first time. They have not yet fixed a date but will join in either March or April 2026, paying the joining fee and the annual fee together on the day they join. How much will they pay in total?

## Answer

The total is **£100 if they join in March 2026, or £110 if they join in April 2026**.

- **Eligibility:** A household membership covers up to two adults and four children living at the same address (`boat-membership.md` §1.2). A family of two adults and two children qualifies.
- **Joining fee:** The one-time fee is £15 (`boat-fees.md` §1.1). The changes register doesn't alter it.
- **Annual household fee:** This depends on the payment date, because the committee raised the fee on 12 December 2025 (`boat-changes.md` §2.2, §2.4).
  - **Paid in March 2026:** The original fee of £85 applies (`boat-fees.md` §1.3).
  - **Paid on or after 1 April 2026:** The revised fee of £95 applies.

| Join date | Joining fee | Annual household fee | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| March 2026 | £15 | £85 | **£100** |
| April 2026 | £15 | £95 | **£110** |

They would save £10 by joining and paying in March. Membership only starts once the Treasurer records cleared payment (`boat-membership.md` §2.3). They should also allow time for the two committee approvals their application needs first (§2.2).
