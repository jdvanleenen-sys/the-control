# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A member returns a borrowed powered hedge trimmer, for which they paid the £20 deposit, with damage the committee confirms was avoidable. The confirmed repair cost is £55. What does the library charge the member for the damage, what happens to the deposit, and when is any balance due?

## Answer

**Damage charge:** £40. Section 3.1 of `lend-refunds.md` charges the actual repair cost for avoidable damage, but caps it at £40 per item. The £55 repair cost is therefore limited to £40.

**Deposit:** The full £20 deposit is kept and applied against the charge. Section 1.2 allows deducting a confirmed repair charge for that tool from its deposit. So nothing is repaid under the 7-day deposit repayment rule in 1.1.

**Balance:** The member owes £20 (£40 minus £20). The library must give them a written calculation (1.2) and written notice of it (3.2). The balance is due within 14 days of that notice.

Until the £20 is paid, it blocks new loans and renewal (`lend-fees.md` 3.2). A pending appeal does not lift that block. Only the committee can waive the charge.

None of the changes in `lend-changes.md` affect these sections.
