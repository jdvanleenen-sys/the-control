# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: On Friday 10 July 2026 an adult member already holds confirmed bookings for sessions on Sunday 12 July 2026 and Tuesday 14 July 2026, both still to come. They now ask to add a third booking for a session on Thursday 16 July 2026. Can the booking be made?

## Answer

**No, the booking can't be made.**

- **Limit that applies:** Normally the weekly limit in `boat-booking.md` §3.1 is three future bookings in any rolling seven-day period. The changes register (`boat-changes.md` §3) replaced that with **two** for bookings dated 1 July to 30 September 2026. The booking is requested on 10 July and is for a session on 16 July. Either date falls inside that window, so the two-booking limit applies.
- **Count:** The member already holds confirmed future bookings on 12 July and 14 July. A session on 16 July would be a third. All three fall within seven days (12–18 July), so the limit would be exceeded.
- **Booking window:** This isn't the obstacle. Bookings for 16 July opened at 8:00 am on 2 July (§2.1).

The member could book a session on 19 July or later, which falls outside the seven-day span that includes 12 July. They could also cancel one of the existing bookings and then take the 16 July session. This assumes the member is active (§1.1), which the question doesn't state.
