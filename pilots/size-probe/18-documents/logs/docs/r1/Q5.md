# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member's friend, who is not a member, has already used club boats as that member's guest on Saturday 14 March, Saturday 2 May and Saturday 20 June 2026. The member wants to bring the same friend as their only guest again on Saturday 11 July 2026. Is that allowed, and if not, when could the friend next use a club boat as a guest?

## Answer

**No, it isn't allowed. The friend could next use a club boat as a guest on Thursday 1 January 2027.**

- **Why not:** Section 4.3 of `boat-membership.md` says a guest may use club boats on no more than three separate days between 1 January and 31 December. The friend has already used three days in 2026: 14 March, 2 May and 20 June. A fourth day on 11 July 2026 would go over that limit.
- **Why the guest limit doesn't help:** Section 4.1 allows up to two guests per visit, but that is a separate rule from the three-day limit in 4.3.
- **Changes register:** `boat-changes.md` doesn't amend section 4.3. Its changes cover fees and the weekly booking limit, so the rule stands as written.
- **Next date:** The count resets on 1 January, and the first day of the new year is Thursday 1 January 2027. The rules don't say the guest must be a member's guest on a Saturday or any other particular day. If the member wants a Saturday, the first one is 2 January 2027.
- **Alternative:** The friend could join the club as a member. The limit applies only to guests, though I haven't checked the eligibility or fee rules for that.
