# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A new household's application is approved and they pay the standard annual fee on Wednesday 1 July 2026, joining that day. Neither named adult collects any item. A written request to cancel and refund the membership reaches the desk on Friday 10 July 2026. Is a refund due, how much, and by when must it be paid?

4. Read reference/lend/lend-fees.md

## Answer

**Yes, a full refund of £30 is due, and it must be paid by Friday 24 July 2026.**

- **Fee:** The household paid on 1 July 2026, which is after 1 January 2026. The replacement in `lend-changes.md` §2 therefore applies, and the standard annual fee is £30, not the original £24 in `lend-fees.md` §2.1.
- **Refund window:** The request reached the desk on 10 July 2026, which is after 1 July 2026. The replacement in `lend-changes.md` §4 therefore applies, and it allows 14 calendar days from joining instead of 7. Joining on 1 July is day zero, so 10 July is day 9. That is inside the window. Under the original 7-day rule it would have been too late (the cutoff was 8 July).
- **Condition:** Neither named adult collected an item, so the "unused membership" condition is met.
- **Payment deadline:** `lend-refunds.md` §2.3 requires an approved refund to be paid within 14 calendar days of receiving the request. Counting from 10 July gives 24 July 2026.

This assumes the household paid the standard fee. If either adult had declared income support, the fee would have been £12 and so would the refund.
