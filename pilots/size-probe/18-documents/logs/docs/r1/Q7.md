# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household reserved a toy and was notified on Friday 27 March 2026 that it is ready for collection. They have not collected it yet and ask how many days the ordinary loan will run from the day they collect it, before any extension. What is the answer?

## Steps (4)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Osterby
3. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md
4. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md

## Answer

**The ordinary loan runs 21 calendar days from the day they collect it.**

- The original rule, `lend-borrowing.md` §2.1, set 14 days.
- The change register (`lend-changes.md` §3) replaced it with 21 days for any item collected on or after 1 April 2026. The test uses the collection date, not the reservation or notification date.
- Their notification on 27 March is before that date, but it doesn't matter. They haven't collected yet, and today is 11 October 2026, so any collection will be after 1 April. The 21-day rule therefore applies.

Under §3.1, collection is due within 7 days of notification, which here means by 3 April 2026. That deadline has passed. The rules I found don't say what happens to a reservation that isn't collected in time. The desk may have released it, so the household should check before turning up.
