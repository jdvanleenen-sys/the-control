# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household returns two items, a toy and a hand saw, on Monday 25 May 2026. Both had a recorded due date of Friday 8 May 2026 and no extension was granted. On the same visit they ask to take out a new toy. What overdue charge is due, and may they take the new toy before paying it?

## Answer

**Overdue charge: £20 in total.**

- Under lend-borrowing.md §3.2, the charge is £1 per item for each calendar day after the recorded due date, capped at £10 per item.
- Both items had a recorded due date of Friday 8 May 2026. Returning them on Monday 25 May makes them 17 days overdue.
- 17 days at £1 comes to £17 per item, which is over the cap. Each item is therefore charged £10, so the toy is £10 and the hand saw is £10.
- The 21-day loan period from lend-changes.md doesn't affect this answer. The due date is already recorded, so I used it as given.
- The hand saw has no deposit, because hand tools don't need one (lend-fees.md §3.1). No deposit deduction is involved.

**New toy: not until the £20 is paid or formally waived.**

- lend-borrowing.md §1.2 says all charges must be paid or formally waived before a loan.
- lend-fees.md §3.2 says an unpaid charge blocks new loans until it is paid or formally waived.
- Only the committee can waive a charge, and it must record the amount and reason in its minutes (§3.3). The desk can't waive it on the spot.
- If the household pays the £20 at the desk during the same visit, they can take the new toy straight away. Their household limit isn't a problem, because both returned items no longer count toward it.
