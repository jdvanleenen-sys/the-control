# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member has a confirmed booking for two rowing boats in the 10:00 session on Sunday 14 June 2026. They arrive at 09:45, pay the boat-use charge for both boats, and at 09:55 the on-duty committee member closes the lake because of lightning, so the session does not go ahead. How much did the member pay, and how much, if anything, is refunded?

## Answer

**The member paid £8 and is refunded the full £8.**

- **Amount paid:** A standard session costs £4 per boat (`boat-fees.md` §3.1). Two boats come to £8. A member may book up to two boats in one session (`boat-booking.md` §3.2). Paying on arrival is fine, because the charge only has to be paid before launch (§3.3).
- **Closure counts as cancellation:** An on-duty committee member may close the lake for lightning (`boat-safety.md` §4.1). Under §4.3, that closure cancels the affected bookings for the purpose of the club-cancellation refund rule.
- **Refund:** The club refunds a boat-use charge in full when it cancels a confirmed booking (`boat-fees.md` §4.1). The booking was confirmed, so the full £8 comes back.
- **Rules that don't apply:** The no-show and 24-hour member-cancellation rules (§4.2–4.3) cover cancellations by the member, not closures by the club. The changes register's shorter cancellation period applies only to payments made on or after 10 August 2026. This payment was on 14 June 2026, and that rule wouldn't change the outcome anyway.
