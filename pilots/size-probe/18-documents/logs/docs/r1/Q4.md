## Answer

The boat-use charge is **£4 in total**, payable before launch.

- **Rate:** Section 3.2 of `boat-fees.md` sets a junior session rate of £2 per boat. It applies when a session is booked solely for junior members and an approved adult supervisor is present. Both juniors (14 and 16) are within the 12–17 junior range, and they are the only people booked.
- **Supervisor:** The named approved adult member stays on site throughout. That meets the on-site requirement in `boat-safety.md` section 28, so I treated them as the approved adult supervisor. The rules don't require the adult to go out in a boat.
- **Boats:** There are two kayaks, so 2 × £2 = £4.
- **Timing:** Section 3.3 requires payment before launch, unless the Treasurer has agreed otherwise in writing.
- **Changes register:** None of the entries in `boat-changes.md` affect the boat-use charge. They cover annual fees, the weekly booking limit and the refund period.
