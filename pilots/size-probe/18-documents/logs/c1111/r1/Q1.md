# Q1, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: A family of two adults and two children, all living at the same address, plan to take out a household membership for the first time. They have not yet fixed a date but will join in either March or April 2026, paying the joining fee and the annual fee together on the day they join. How much will they pay in total?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/boat/boat-fees.md
2. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md
3. Grep reference/boat/boat-membership.md

## Answer

It depends on the payment date.

- **If they pay in March 2026:** £100. That is the £15 joining fee plus the £85 household annual fee (boat-fees.md, 1.1 and 1.3).
- **If they pay in April 2026:** £110. That is the £15 joining fee plus the £95 household annual fee. The changes register replaces boat-fees.md 1.3 for payments made on or after 1 April 2026 (boat-changes.md, 2.2 and 2.4).

Two adults and two children living at the same address fit a household membership (boat-membership.md, 1.2). The joining fee is the same in both months (boat-fees.md, 1.1).
