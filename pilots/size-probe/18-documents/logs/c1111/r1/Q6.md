# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household's membership expired on Saturday 20 December 2025. Neither named adult declares income support and the household has no unpaid charges. One of the adults comes to the desk on Saturday 10 January 2026 and the desk accepts their renewal payment that day. How much do they pay, and from what date does the renewed membership run?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-fees.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
3. Read reference/lend/lend-membership.md

## Answer

They pay £30, and the renewed membership runs from Saturday 10 January 2026.

- **Fee:** The payment was accepted on 10 January 2026, which is on or after 1 January 2026. So the replacement annual fee of £30 applies instead of £24 (lend-changes.md, section 2, replacing lend-fees.md 2.1). The £12 reduced fee doesn't apply because neither named adult declares income support (lend-fees.md, 2.2).
- **Start date:** Renewal starts on the date the renewal payment is accepted, not on the old expiry date (lend-membership.md, 2.1). The term is 12 calendar months from that date.
