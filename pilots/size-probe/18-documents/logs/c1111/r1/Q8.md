# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household returns two items, a toy and a hand saw, on Monday 25 May 2026. Both had a recorded due date of Friday 8 May 2026 and no extension was granted. On the same visit they ask to take out a new toy. What overdue charge is due, and may they take the new toy before paying it?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md

## Answer

The overdue charge is £20, and no, they may not take the new toy until it is paid or formally waived.

**Charge:** Each item is charged £1 per calendar day after its recorded due date, capped at £10 per item (lend-borrowing.md, 3.2). Both items were 17 days overdue (9 to 25 May), so each reaches the £10 cap. That gives £10 for the toy and £10 for the hand saw, £20 in total. The changes document does not replace section 3.2.

**New toy:** Borrowing requires that all charges are paid or formally waived (lend-borrowing.md, 1.2).
