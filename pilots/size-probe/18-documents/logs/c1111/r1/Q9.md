# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A new household's application is approved and they pay the standard annual fee on Wednesday 1 July 2026, joining that day. Neither named adult collects any item. A written request to cancel and refund the membership reaches the desk on Friday 10 July 2026. Is a refund due, how much, and by when must it be paid?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-refunds.md

## Answer

**Yes, a refund is due. It is the full annual fee of £30, and it must be paid by Friday 24 July 2026.**

- **Refund due:** The request reached the desk on 10 July 2026, which is on or after 1 July 2026, so the replacement 2.1 in the changes register applies (lend-changes.md, section 4). It refunds the full annual fee if a written request reaches the desk within 14 calendar days of joining, and neither named adult has collected any item. Joining on 1 July is day zero, so 10 July is day 9, within the 14 days. No item was collected.
- **Amount:** The fee paid on 1 July 2026 falls under the replacement fee text of £30 per 12-month term for a household (lend-changes.md, section 2).
- **Deadline:** An approved membership refund must be paid within 14 calendar days of receiving the request (lend-refunds.md, section 2.3). Counting from 10 July, that is 24 July 2026.
