# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A member returns a borrowed powered hedge trimmer, for which they paid the £20 deposit, with damage the committee confirms was avoidable. The confirmed repair cost is £55. What does the library charge the member for the damage, what happens to the deposit, and when is any balance due?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-refunds.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md

## Answer

**The library charges £40.** Section 3.1 charges the actual repair cost for avoidable damage, capped at £40 per item. The £55 repair cost is therefore limited to £40 (lend-refunds.md, 3.1).

**The £20 deposit is deducted in full.** Section 1.2 allows a confirmed repair charge for that tool to be deducted from its deposit. The member gets a written calculation, and any balance owed remains payable (lend-refunds.md, 1.2). The £20 deposit leaves a £20 balance of the £40 charge, and no deposit is left to repay.

**The £20 balance is due within 14 days of the written notice of the calculation** (lend-refunds.md, 3.2).

The changes document doesn't replace any of these sections.
