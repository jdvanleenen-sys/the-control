# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member has a confirmed booking for two rowing boats in the 10:00 session on Sunday 14 June 2026. They arrive at 09:45, pay the boat-use charge for both boats, and at 09:55 the on-duty committee member closes the lake because of lightning, so the session does not go ahead. How much did the member pay, and how much, if anything, is refunded?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/boat/boat-fees.md
2. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md
3. Read reference/boat/boat-booking.md
4. Read reference/boat/boat-safety.md

## Answer

The member paid £8 and is refunded £8, the full amount.

- **Paid:** The standard session charge is £4 per boat, and two boats were booked (boat-fees.md, 3.1). That makes £8. The booking was for an adult member, so the junior rate does not apply (3.2).
- **Refund:** A closure by an on-duty committee member cancels the affected bookings for the purpose of boat-fees.md 4.1 (boat-safety.md, 4.1 and 4.3). The club refunds a boat-use charge in full when it cancels a confirmed booking (boat-fees.md, 4.1).

The changes register does not alter this. Its only change to a refund rule is the 12-hour cancellation period, and that applies to member cancellations (boat-changes.md, 4).
