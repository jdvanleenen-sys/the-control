# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: On Friday 10 July 2026 an adult member already holds confirmed bookings for sessions on Sunday 12 July 2026 and Tuesday 14 July 2026, both still to come. They now ask to add a third booking for a session on Thursday 16 July 2026. Can the booking be made?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/boat/boat-booking.md
2. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md

## Answer

No. The booking can't be made, because the member would hold three future bookings in a seven-day period and the limit is two.

The changes register replaces the weekly limit for bookings dated 1 July to 30 September 2026. The new rule is: "A member may hold no more than two future bookings in any rolling seven-day period" (boat-changes.md, section 3.3, replacing boat-booking.md, section 3.1). The session dates (12, 14 and 16 July) and the day of the request (10 July) both fall within that range. The three sessions fit inside one rolling seven-day period, 12 to 18 July.
