# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household reserved a toy and was notified on Friday 27 March 2026 that it is ready for collection. They have not collected it yet and ask how many days the ordinary loan will run from the day they collect it, before any extension. What is the answer?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md

## Answer

It depends on the collection date, which hasn't happened yet.

- **Collected on or before 31 March 2026:** the ordinary loan is 14 calendar days from collection (lend-borrowing.md, 2.1 Ordinary loan).
- **Collected on or after 1 April 2026:** the ordinary loan is 21 calendar days from collection. The changes register replaces 2.1 for collections from that date, and it tests the collection date, not the reservation or notification date (lend-changes.md, section 3).

Collection is due within 7 days of notification (lend-borrowing.md, 3.1), so by 3 April 2026. A collection on 1, 2 or 3 April would get 21 days.
