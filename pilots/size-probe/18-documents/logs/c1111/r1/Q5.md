# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member's friend, who is not a member, has already used club boats as that member's guest on Saturday 14 March, Saturday 2 May and Saturday 20 June 2026. The member wants to bring the same friend as their only guest again on Saturday 11 July 2026. Is that allowed, and if not, when could the friend next use a club boat as a guest?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep reference/boat
2. Read reference/boat/boat-membership.md
3. Grep reference/boat/boat-changes.md

## Answer

No, it isn't allowed. The friend can next use a club boat as a guest on Friday 1 January 2027.

The rule allows a guest no more than three separate days of club-boat use between 1 January and 31 December (boat-membership.md, section 4.3). The friend has already used boats on three separate days in 2026: 14 March, 2 May and 20 June. A fourth day on 11 July 2026 would go over that limit.

The limit runs by calendar year, so the count starts again on 1 January 2027. The changes document (boat-changes.md) does not replace section 4.3.
