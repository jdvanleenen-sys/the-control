# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: Two junior members, aged 14 and 16, are the only people booked into the 14:00 session on Wednesday 20 May 2026, with one kayak each. An approved adult member is named in the booking and stays on site throughout but does not go out in a boat. What boat-use charge must be paid before launch for that session?

2. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md

4. Grep reference/boat

## Answer

**The charge is £4.** That is the junior session rate of £2 per boat for the two kayaks, payable before launch.

- **Rate:** A session booked solely for junior members costs £2 per boat when an approved adult supervisor is present (boat-fees.md, 3.2). Both boaters are juniors and the adult is named and on site, so this rate applies. For juniors aged 12 to 17, boat-safety.md requires only that an approved adult member remains on site. Two kayaks at £2 each come to £4.
- **Timing:** Boat-use charges must be paid before launch unless the Treasurer has agreed otherwise in writing (boat-fees.md, 3.3).
- **Changes register:** boat-changes.md does not replace sections 3.2 or 3.3.
