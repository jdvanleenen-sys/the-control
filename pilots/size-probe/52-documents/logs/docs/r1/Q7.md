# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household reserved a toy and was notified on Friday 27 March 2026 that it is ready for collection. They have not collected it yet and ask how many days the ordinary loan will run from the day they collect it, before any extension. What is the answer?

## Steps (4)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Osterby
3. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md
4. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md

## Answer

It depends on the day they collect it, because a rule change takes effect on 1 April 2026.

- **Collected Saturday 28 March to Tuesday 31 March 2026:** the ordinary loan is **14 calendar days**. This is the original rule in section 2.1 of `lend-borrowing.md`.
- **Collected Wednesday 1 April to Friday 3 April 2026:** the ordinary loan is **21 calendar days**. The 16 March 2026 decision in section 3 of `lend-changes.md` replaces the old section 2.1 for items collected on or after 1 April. It tests the collection date, not the reservation or notification date.

Collection is due within 7 days of notification (section 3.1 of `lend-borrowing.md`). Counting from 27 March, the last day is 3 April 2026, so those are the only collection dates the rules cover. The rules don't say whether the 7 days include the notification day. Today is 11 October 2026, so that window has long passed. The rules don't say what happens to an uncollected reservation after that.

If you tell me the actual collection date, I can give a single figure.
