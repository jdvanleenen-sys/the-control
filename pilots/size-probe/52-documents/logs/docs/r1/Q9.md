# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A new household's application is approved and they pay the standard annual fee on Wednesday 1 July 2026, joining that day. Neither named adult collects any item. A written request to cancel and refund the membership reaches the desk on Friday 10 July 2026. Is a refund due, how much, and by when must it be paid?

## Answer

**Yes, a full refund of £30 is due, and it must be paid by Friday 24 July 2026.**

- **Eligibility:** The request reached the desk on 10 July 2026, which is on or after 1 July 2026. That means the replacement in `lend-changes.md` §4 applies, not the original 7-day rule in `lend-refunds.md` §2.1. The new rule gives 14 calendar days from joining, with the joining date as day zero. Joining on 1 July makes 10 July day 9, so the request is in time. No item was collected, so the "unused" condition is met.
- **Amount:** The fee was accepted on 1 July 2026, which is on or after 1 January 2026. The standard annual fee is therefore £30 (`lend-changes.md` §2), not the original £24 in `lend-fees.md` §2.1. The full annual fee is refunded, so the refund is £30.
- **Deadline:** `lend-refunds.md` §2.3 says to pay an approved refund within 14 calendar days of receiving the request. Counting from 10 July 2026 gives 24 July 2026.
