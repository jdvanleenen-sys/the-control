# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: On Friday 10 July 2026 an adult member already holds confirmed bookings for sessions on Sunday 12 July 2026 and Tuesday 14 July 2026, both still to come. They now ask to add a third booking for a session on Thursday 16 July 2026. Can the booking be made?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep Brindlecombe
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/boat/boat-booking.md
4. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md

## Answer

**No, the booking can't be made.**

- **Which limit applies:** Section 3.1 of `boat-booking.md` allows three future bookings in a rolling seven-day period. The changes register (`boat-changes.md`, §3) replaces it with a limit of **two** for bookings dated 1 July to 30 September 2026. The register says the booking date decides whether an entry applies. The request is made on 10 July and the session is on 16 July. Both dates are inside the summer period, so the two-booking limit applies however "booking date" is read.
- **Count:** The 12, 14 and 16 July sessions all fall within one seven-day span (12–18 July). The member already holds two future bookings in that span, so a third would be over the limit.
- **Booking window:** This isn't the problem. Bookings for 16 July opened at 8:00 am on 2 July (§2.1), so the window is open.

The member could book a session on 19 July or later. Under the rolling rule, that session would fall outside any seven-day span that also holds both existing bookings. They could also cancel one of the existing bookings and book 16 July instead.

I've assumed the member is active, since only active members can book (§1.1).
