## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A member returns a borrowed powered hedge trimmer, for which they paid the £20 deposit, with damage the committee confirms was avoidable. The confirmed repair cost is £55. What does the library charge the member for the damage, what happens to the deposit, and when is any balance due?

## Answer

**Charge for the damage:** The library charges the actual repair cost for avoidable damage, but capped at £40 per item (lend-refunds.md §3.1). The repair cost is £55, so the member is charged **£40**. The other £15 is not recoverable.

**The deposit:** The £20 deposit is applied against that £40 charge. Only a confirmed repair or replacement charge for that tool can be deducted from the deposit (§1.2). The member therefore gets no deposit back. The member must receive a written calculation (§1.2).

**Balance and timing:** The balance is £40 − £20 = **£20**. It stays payable by the member (§1.2). It is due within 14 days of the written notice of the calculation (§3.2). The 14 days run from the notice date, not from the return date.

Other points:
- Any overdue charges are separate and come on top of this.
- The member can appeal under lend-conduct.md. An appeal that is still pending does not lift the block on new loans and renewals while the charge is unpaid (lend-fees.md §3.2).
- Only the committee can waive the charge, and it must record the amount and reason in its minutes (lend-fees.md §3.3).
- The rule changes register (lend-changes.md) doesn't alter any of the sections used here.
