# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member has a confirmed booking for two rowing boats in the 10:00 session on Sunday 14 June 2026. They arrive at 09:45, pay the boat-use charge for both boats, and at 09:55 the on-duty committee member closes the lake because of lightning, so the session does not go ahead. How much did the member pay, and how much, if anything, is refunded?

## Answer

The member paid **£8** and is owed a **full refund of £8**.

- **Charge:** Boat-use charges are £4 per boat for a standard session (boat-fees.md §3.1). The member has two boats, which is allowed (boat-booking.md §3.2). That makes 2 × £4 = £8. The junior rate doesn't apply to an adult member.
- **Refund:** The on-duty committee member closed the lake because of lightning, which they are allowed to do (boat-safety.md §4.1). A closure counts as the club cancelling the affected bookings (boat-safety.md §4.3). The club must then refund the boat-use charge in full when it cancels a confirmed booking (boat-fees.md §4.1).

The later changes in the register don't alter this. The 12-hour cancellation rule only replaces the rule for cancellations made by the member (§4.2), and this was a club cancellation. The member's arrival at 09:45 and the 09:55 closure don't affect the refund either. The member had already paid before launch, as §3.3 requires.
