# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household returns two items, a toy and a hand saw, on Monday 25 May 2026. Both had a recorded due date of Friday 8 May 2026 and no extension was granted. On the same visit they ask to take out a new toy. What overdue charge is due, and may they take the new toy before paying it?

## Steps (6)

1. Grep Osterby
2. Glob **/*{rule,Rule,library,Library,osterby,Osterby}*
3. Read reference/lend/lend-fees.md
4. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md
5. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
6. Read reference/lend/lend-desk-guide.md

## Answer

**The overdue charge is £20, and they can't take the new toy until it's paid or formally waived.**

**Charge**
- Both items were due on 8 May 2026. By Monday 25 May they are 17 calendar days overdue (9 days left in May after the 8th, plus 8 days in the second half).
- Under lend-borrowing.md §3.2, the charge is £1 per item for each calendar day after the recorded due date, capped at £10 per item.
- At 17 days, each item hits the cap: £17 becomes £10.
- Two items at £10 each comes to **£20**.
- The 14-day versus 21-day loan period doesn't change this. Both items already have a recorded due date, and lend-changes.md doesn't alter the overdue charge.

**New toy**
- lend-borrowing.md §1.2 says all charges must be paid or formally waived before a household can borrow.
- lend-fees.md §3.2 says an unpaid charge blocks new loans until it is paid or formally waived. A pending appeal doesn't lift that block.
- Only the committee can waive a charge, and it must record the amount and reason in its minutes (lend-fees.md §3.3). Desk staff can't waive it.
- The household can pay the £20 at the desk (cash or card) on the same visit and then take out the new toy. Their membership must also be active, and they'd be within the 4-item limit once the two returns are processed.
