# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member's friend, who is not a member, has already used club boats as that member's guest on Saturday 14 March, Saturday 2 May and Saturday 20 June 2026. The member wants to bring the same friend as their only guest again on Saturday 11 July 2026. Is that allowed, and if not, when could the friend next use a club boat as a guest?

## Answer

**No, it isn't allowed. The friend can next use a club boat as a guest on Thursday 1 January 2027.**

Section 4.3 of `boat-membership.md` says a guest may use club boats on no more than three separate days between 1 January and 31 December. The friend has already used boats on 14 March, 2 May and 20 June 2026, which is three separate days. A fourth day on 11 July 2026 would go over the limit.

The limit runs by calendar year, so it resets on 1 January 2027. The changes register (`boat-changes.md`) doesn't amend section 4.3.

Bringing the friend as the only guest doesn't help, because the two-guest limit in section 4.1 is a separate rule. The cap in 4.3 applies to each guest individually.

The friend could still come on 11 July as a non-boating visitor. I didn't check whether the rules allow that.
