# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household's membership expired on Saturday 20 December 2025. Neither named adult declares income support and the household has no unpaid charges. One of the adults comes to the desk on Saturday 10 January 2026 and the desk accepts their renewal payment that day. How much do they pay, and from what date does the renewed membership run?

1. Glob **/*

## Answer

They pay **£30**, and the renewed membership runs **from Saturday 10 January 2026**.

- **Fee:** The original standard fee was £24 (lend-fees.md §2.1). The register in lend-changes.md §2 replaces it with £30 for any renewal payment accepted on or after 1 January 2026. The desk accepts this payment on 10 January 2026, so £30 applies. The £12 reduced fee doesn't apply because neither adult declares income support.
- **Start date:** Under lend-membership.md §2.1, renewal starts on the date the payment is accepted, not on the old expiry date. The lapse from 20 December 2025 is not back-dated, so the membership starts on 10 January 2026. It lasts 12 calendar months, so it ends on 9 January 2027 (the day before the anniversary). The rules don't state that end date explicitly.
- **Renewal block:** The household has no unpaid charges, so nothing blocks the renewal (lend-membership.md §2.2, lend-fees.md §3.2).

The front-desk guide still shows £24, but it says it is out of date and that the governing files and the changes register prevail.
