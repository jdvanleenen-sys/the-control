## Answer

**The charge is £4 in total, paid before launch.** That is £2 for each of the two kayaks.

- **Rate:** Section 3.2 of `boat-fees.md` sets a junior session rate of £2 per boat. It applies to a session booked solely for junior members when an approved adult supervisor is present. Both juniors are the only people booked, so the session is junior-only. The standard £4 per boat rate in 3.1 doesn't apply.
- **Supervisor:** The named approved adult stays on site throughout, which meets the supervisor condition. This matches `boat-safety.md` 2.2, which only requires an approved adult to remain on site for juniors aged 12 to 17. Both juniors, aged 14 and 16, are in that range. The rules don't say the adult has to go out in a boat.
- **Timing:** Under 3.3 the charge must be paid before launch, unless the Treasurer has agreed otherwise in writing.
- **Changes register:** Nothing in `boat-changes.md` affects section 3.2. Its entries only cover annual fees, the weekly booking limit and the member cancellation refund period.
