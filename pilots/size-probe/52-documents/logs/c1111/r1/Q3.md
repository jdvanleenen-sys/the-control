# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: On Friday 10 July 2026 an adult member already holds confirmed bookings for sessions on Sunday 12 July 2026 and Tuesday 14 July 2026, both still to come. They now ask to add a third booking for a session on Thursday 16 July 2026. Can the booking be made?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md
2. Read reference/boat/boat-booking.md

## Answer

No. The booking can't be made, because it would be a third future booking in a seven-day period and the limit is two.

The summer limit says "A member may hold no more than two future bookings in any rolling seven-day period" (boat-changes.md, section 3.3, replacing boat-booking.md, section 3.1). It applies to bookings dated 1 July to 30 September 2026 (boat-changes.md, section 3.2). The booking date is 10 July 2026, and the session date of 16 July also falls in that range. The sessions on 12, 14 and 16 July all fall within one rolling seven-day period.
