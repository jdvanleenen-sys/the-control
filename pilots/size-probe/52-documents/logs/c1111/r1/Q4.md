# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: Two junior members, aged 14 and 16, are the only people booked into the 14:00 session on Wednesday 20 May 2026, with one kayak each. An approved adult member is named in the booking and stays on site throughout but does not go out in a boat. What boat-use charge must be paid before launch for that session?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/boat/boat-fees.md
2. Read reference/boat/boat-changes.md
3. Grep reference/boat

## Answer

The charge is £4 in total, which is £2 for each of the two kayaks.

The session is booked solely for junior members and an approved adult is present, so the junior rate of £2 per boat applies (boat-fees.md, 3.2). That rate is payable before launch (boat-fees.md, 3.3). No entry in boat-changes.md replaces section 3.2 or 3.3.

The rules require an approved adult member to be named in the booking and to stay on site while juniors use boats (boat-booking.md, 1.2; boat-safety.md, 2.2). Section 3.2 says "approved adult supervisor is present" and the documents don't define "supervisor". I've treated the named adult who stays on site as meeting it. If "supervisor" meant something more, the standard £4 per boat would apply, giving £8 (boat-fees.md, 3.1).
