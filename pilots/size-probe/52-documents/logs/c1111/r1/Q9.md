# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A new household's application is approved and they pay the standard annual fee on Wednesday 1 July 2026, joining that day. Neither named adult collects any item. A written request to cancel and refund the membership reaches the desk on Friday 10 July 2026. Is a refund due, how much, and by when must it be paid?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-refunds.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
3. Read reference/lend/lend-fees.md

## Answer

Yes, a full refund of £30 is due, and it must be paid by Friday 24 July 2026.

- **Refund due:** The request reached the desk on 10 July 2026, which is on or after 1 July 2026. The changes entry therefore replaces the original rule. It gives a full refund if a written request reaches the desk within 14 calendar days of joining, and the joining date is day zero. The request arrived on day 9, and neither named adult collected an item (lend-changes.md, section 4, replacing lend-refunds.md, 2.1).
- **Amount:** The refund is the full annual fee. The fee was paid on 1 July 2026, on or after 1 January 2026, so it is the standard £30 (lend-changes.md, section 2, replacing lend-fees.md, 2.1).
- **Deadline:** An approved membership refund must be paid within 14 calendar days of receiving the request. Counting from 10 July 2026, that is 24 July 2026 (lend-refunds.md, 2.3).
