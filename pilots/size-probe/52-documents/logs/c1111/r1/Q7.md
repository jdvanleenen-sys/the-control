# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household reserved a toy and was notified on Friday 27 March 2026 that it is ready for collection. They have not collected it yet and ask how many days the ordinary loan will run from the day they collect it, before any extension. What is the answer?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md

## Answer

It depends on the collection date, which the question doesn't give. Notification on 27 March 2026 doesn't decide it.

- If they collect on or after 1 April 2026, the ordinary loan is 21 calendar days from collection (lend-changes.md, section 3, which replaces lend-borrowing.md 2.1).
- If they collect before 1 April 2026, it is 14 calendar days from collection (lend-borrowing.md, section 2.1).

Collection is due within 7 days of notification (lend-borrowing.md, 3.1). That puts the last day to collect at 3 April 2026. I did not work out which collection dates the rule allows beyond that.
