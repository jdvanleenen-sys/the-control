# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A household returns two items, a toy and a hand saw, on Monday 25 May 2026. Both had a recorded due date of Friday 8 May 2026 and no extension was granted. On the same visit they ask to take out a new toy. What overdue charge is due, and may they take the new toy before paying it?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-borrowing.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md

## Answer

**Overdue charge:** £20 in total, which is £10 for the toy and £10 for the hand saw.

The charge is £1 per item for each calendar day after the recorded due date, capped at £10 per item (lend-borrowing.md, 3.2). From 8 May to 25 May is 17 days. That would be £17 per item, so the £10 cap applies to each. The changes register does not replace 3.2.

**New toy:** No, not before paying. Borrowing requires that all charges be paid or formally waived (lend-borrowing.md, 1.2).
