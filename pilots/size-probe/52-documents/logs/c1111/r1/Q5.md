# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Brindlecombe Community Boat Club rules: An adult member's friend, who is not a member, has already used club boats as that member's guest on Saturday 14 March, Saturday 2 May and Saturday 20 June 2026. The member wants to bring the same friend as their only guest again on Saturday 11 July 2026. Is that allowed, and if not, when could the friend next use a club boat as a guest?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep reference/boat
2. Read reference/boat/boat-membership.md

## Answer

No, it isn't allowed. The friend has already used club boats as a guest on three separate days in 2026 (14 March, 2 May and 20 June). A guest may use club boats on no more than three separate days between 1 January and 31 December (boat-membership.md, section 4.3). The next day would be a fourth in the same calendar year.

The friend could next use a club boat as a guest on or after Friday 1 January 2027, when the new 1 January–31 December period starts (boat-membership.md, section 4.3).

The changes document (boat-changes.md) doesn't replace section 4.3.
