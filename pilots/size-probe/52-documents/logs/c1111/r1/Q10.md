# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Osterby Toy and Tool Library rules: A member returns a borrowed powered hedge trimmer, for which they paid the £20 deposit, with damage the committee confirms was avoidable. The confirmed repair cost is £55. What does the library charge the member for the damage, what happens to the deposit, and when is any balance due?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/lend/lend-refunds.md
2. Read reference/lend/lend-changes.md
3. Read reference/lend/lend-fees.md

## Answer

The library charges £40 for the damage. The whole £20 deposit is deducted from that, and the £20 balance is due within 14 days of the written notice.

- **Charge:** Avoidable damage is charged at actual repair cost, up to £40 per item. The £55 repair cost is capped at £40 (lend-refunds.md, 3.1).
- **Deposit:** The confirmed repair charge for that tool is deducted from its deposit. The member gets a written calculation, and any balance owed remains payable (lend-refunds.md, 1.2).
- **Balance due:** Payment is due within 14 days of the written notice of the calculation (lend-refunds.md, 3.2).
