# Q8, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium. The page showed a thinking label before the answer: "Checking membership rules and fee updates for village group eligibility."

## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

No, it doesn’t qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2).

The room-hire charge is £48: Main Hall at £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1) for two hours. The higher £28 rate in hall-changes.md applies only to events starting on or after 1 November 2025, so it doesn’t apply to an event on 15 October 2025.
