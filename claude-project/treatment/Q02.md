# Q2, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium.

## Prompt

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

The Fenwray Film Society changes register replaced the adult fee from 1 July 2025, and the date the treasurer receives payment decides which fee applies (film-changes.md, section 1, entries 1.2 to 1.4).

First person (received 30 June 2025): the payment came before 1 July 2025, so the old fee of £24 stands (film-changes.md, 1.4; film-fees.md, 1.1).
Second person (received 2 July 2025): the payment came on or after 1 July 2025, so the fee is £28 (film-changes.md, 1.2).
