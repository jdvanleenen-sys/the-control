# Q6, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium.

## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Answer

The equipment charge is £25 (hall-fees.md, section 4).

Portable speaker: £10 per booking (4.2)
Folding display boards: £5 per set per booking (4.3), so 3 sets cost £15

Total: £10 + £15 = £25. The booking date of 6 September 2025 is on or after the 1 April 2025 start date for these charges (hall-fees.md, 1.2), and hall-changes.md does not replace sections 4.2 or 4.3.
