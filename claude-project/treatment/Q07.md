# Q7, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium.

## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

No. Amplified music on a Saturday must stop by 21:30 for an event on 4 April 2026 (hall-changes.md, section 5, replacing hall-conduct.md, section 4.1). The 22:00 cutoff the hirer cites was replaced from 1 April 2026, and the controlling date is the day the music is played, which is after that effective date.
