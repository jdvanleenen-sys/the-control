# Q4, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium. The page showed a thinking label before the answer: "Working out membership rules and applicable admission pricing."

## Prompt

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

No, they are not entitled to the member price. A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, section 5.4), and 8 November 2025 falls within the suspension period.

They pay the standard admission price of £7 (film-fees.md, section 2.1).
