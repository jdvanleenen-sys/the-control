# Q9, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium. The page showed a thinking label before the answer: "Calculating total attendance against room capacity limits."

## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

Attendance: Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md, 4.2), and that figure includes organisers and children (4.4). With 16 children and 5 adults, the total is 21.

Supervision: No, the children may not be left without adult supervision. Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, 3.3), and all the children here are aged 8 to 11. So at least some of the adults must stay with them while the others prepare the other room. The rules set no adult-to-child ratio.
