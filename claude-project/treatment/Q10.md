# Q10, in the project that holds the folder

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium. The page showed a thinking label before the answer: "Checking trustee quorum and remote attendance rules for gaps."

## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Answer

The documents don’t say, so I can’t give a yes or no on either point.

The quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). The rules expressly do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). They therefore don’t settle whether a trustee on a video call counts towards those four, and so don’t settle whether the meeting would be quorate with three in person and one remote. No entry in hall-changes.md alters these sections.
