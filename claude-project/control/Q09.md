The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

If you paste the relevant sections (or upload the file), I can work through it with you. For your scenario, these are the provisions to look for:

Meeting Room capacity. Is there a maximum occupancy, and does it count children and adults together? Here that would be 21 people in total.
Rules for children’s events. Many halls set a minimum adult-to-child ratio, sometimes varying by age band (8 to 11 is often treated differently from under-8s). Sixteen children with five adults is a ratio of about 1:3.2, which would be well within most common ratios, but the hall’s own figure is what matters.
Supervision requirements. Look for wording on whether children may ever be left without an adult present, whether a named responsible adult must stay in the room, and whether any adults need DBS checks or safeguarding training.
Booking conditions. The rules may require the hirer to be over a certain age, to declare the nature of the event, or to have insurance for children’s activities.
Date-specific restrictions. Check whether 19 October 2025 raises any issue, such as a prior booking or a rule about hours of use.
