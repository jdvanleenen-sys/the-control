# Q5, in a plain chat with no project

Model shown by the product: Sonnet 5.5, Medium.

## Prompt

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

I don’t have the Fenwray Film Society’s rules, so I can’t tell you the correct price, and I don’t want to guess at a figure.

The volunteer’s old price list is probably not a reliable basis, though. If it’s out of date, the £2 under-12 rate may have been replaced or changed. The current rules would also likely settle some points that could change the answer:

Which price list is in force on 16 August 2025. Rules often have effective dates or amendments, so the answer depends on what applied on that date.
How the child category is defined. Some societies set the age cutoff differently (under 12, under 16, and so on) or tie it to a ticket type.
Whether accompaniment matters. The rules may offer a free or discounted child ticket when a paying adult attends, or require an adult for certain screenings.
Whether public screenings differ from member screenings. Pricing can vary between the two.

If you paste the relevant rule text, including any amendments or effective dates, I’ll work out the answer from it. Otherwise, I’d check with the society’s committee or the most recently published pricing rather than relying on the old list.
