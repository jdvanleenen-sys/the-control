## Prompt

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

On the facts you’ve given, yes. 21:45 is earlier than the 22:00 cut-off for Friday and Saturday amplified music, and 4 April 2026 is a Saturday, so the contract’s finish time fits inside the general rule.

I don’t have the Quillbrook Village Hall rules, so I’m relying on your description of them. Before treating it as settled, check these in the actual text:

Special-date exceptions. 4 April 2026 is Easter Saturday, so look for any holiday or bank-holiday provisions that might shorten or change the usual hours.
Overriding conditions. The rules may say the 22:00 limit is subject to the premises licence, noise limits, or committee approval for amplified events.
Other end-time requirements. For example, the hall may need to be cleared or closed by a set time, which could affect when the band must stop or pack up.
Hirer-specific restrictions. Some halls apply stricter terms to certain bookings or hirers.

If none of those apply, the band may play until 21:45. If you can paste the relevant clauses, I can check them against the booking.
