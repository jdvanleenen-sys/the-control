## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**It doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Section 3.2 of `hall-fees.md` says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. Registration alone isn't enough, and nine members is one short. Under section 3.5, no other discount can stand in for it.
- **Rate:** The committee raised the Main Hall rate to £28 an hour, but only for events starting on or after 1 November 2025 (`hall-changes.md` section 2). A 15 October 2025 event is before that date, so the original £24 an hour in section 2.1 applies.
- **Charge:** 2 hours × £24 = **£48**. Two hours is also the minimum paid booking (section 2.4).

The £48 covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit also applies, and it counts towards the total. Kitchen or equipment charges would be extra.
