> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Which fee applies:** The fee depends on the date the treasurer receives the payment (changes register, 1.3). Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee of £24 (1.4). From 1 July 2025, annual adult membership costs £28 (1.2). This replaces section 1.1 of `film-fees.md`.
- **First person:** Their payment was received on 30 June 2025, so the old £24 fee applies.
- **Second person:** Their payment was received on 2 July 2025, so the new £28 fee applies.

The dates of acceptance don't change this. Payment is due only after an application is accepted (`film-membership.md` 2.5), and it counts as received when cash is handed over or a transfer clears (`film-fees.md` 4.5).
