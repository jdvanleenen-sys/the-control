## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. They pay £7, the standard admission price.**

- **Suspension:** Their membership is still active, but they're suspended from 1 to 20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside the suspension. Under `film-membership.md` §5.4, a suspended member cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices. The suspension is 20 days, within the 30-day maximum in `film-conduct.md` §4.1.
- **Price:** With the member price unavailable, standard admission applies under `film-fees.md` §2.1, which is £7 per screening. Their age of 35 gives no concession, because the child rates only cover people under 16.
- **The £5 member price doesn't apply either:** The committee raised the member price from £4 to £5 for screenings from 1 February 2026 (`film-changes.md` §3). It has no effect here because the screening is on 8 November 2025, and the member price wasn't available to this person anyway.

They also need to meet the usual entry conditions, such as the capacity limit. The audience cap on 8 November 2025 is still 42, because the reduction to 36 only starts on 1 June 2026.
