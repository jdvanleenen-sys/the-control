# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Quillbrook
3. Grep "amplif|music|22:00|21:45|curfew" in reference/hall
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Read reference/hall/hall-conduct.md

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is too late.**

- The original rule, hall-conduct.md §4.1, set the Friday and Saturday cutoff at 22:00. The hirer is relying on that wording.
- The Committee Changes Register replaced §4.1 (hall-changes.md §5). The committee decided this on 20 March 2026, and it took effect on 1 April 2026. From then on, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played (§5.3). That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies.
- The band's contract is a private agreement between the band and the hirer. It doesn't override the hall's rules.

The hirer should have the band finish by 21:30. They could also ask the committee about this, since the contract's 21:45 time doesn't fit the current rule.

Note that hall-quick-guide.md also gives 21:30 for Friday and Saturday.
