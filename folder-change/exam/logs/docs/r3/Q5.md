## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

The correct ticket price is **£3**.

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children under 12 pay £3 when an adult accompanies them. The fees document was effective from 1 March 2025, so it covers the 16 August 2025 screening. A 9-year-old with a parent who stays with them throughout meets both conditions.
- **The volunteer's £2 price:** That comes from `film-fees-archive.md` (section 2.3). That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025 and says it must not be used for current charges.
- **Later amendments:** The committee changes register has no change to the under-12 price. Its only ticket change is the members' price, from 1 February 2026, which doesn't affect this case.
