## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`):

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40** on 18 November 2025. Rule 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start, and the advertised start is 19:30.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**, which is 30 minutes before the advertised start (rule 3.3).

The committee changes register doesn't amend either rule. Its only change to the screening rules is a lower maximum audience, which doesn't affect this question.

If the person arrives after the film has started, rule 3.5 says they can enter only during a suitable break.
