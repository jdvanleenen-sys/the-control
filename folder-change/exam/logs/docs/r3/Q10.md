# Q10, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep quorum
2. Glob **/*uillbrook*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md

## Answer

**The rules don't say, so they don't clearly allow it. Whether the meeting is quorate depends on a point the rules leave open.**

- **Quorum:** Rule 4.2 says "Four trustees form a quorum." Four trustees are planning to attend, so quorum is met only if the remote trustee counts.
- **Remote attendance:** Rule 6.3 says the rules "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely." Nothing in them either permits or bars a video-call attendee from counting. Rule 4.3 refers to those "present and voting", but the rules don't define "present".

**Outcome:** With three trustees in person, the quorum is certain to be met only if the remote trustee counts. The rules don't settle that. If the meeting went ahead and decisions rested on that fourth person, their validity could be challenged.

**Safer options:**
1. Get a fourth trustee to attend in person. The committee has five to nine trustees (rule 2.1), so at least one other should be available.
2. Before 15 November, have the committee agree and minute that remote attendees count towards quorum. This is a reasonable reading, but it is a judgement the rules don't make for you.
3. Check whether the hall has a wider governing document, such as a constitution, or legal requirements that cover remote attendance. I only had these committee rules to go on.
