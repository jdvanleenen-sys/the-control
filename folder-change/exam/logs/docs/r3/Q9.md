# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that figure includes organisers, performers and children (hall-bookings.md 4.2 and 4.4). Sixteen children plus five adults makes 21, which is three under the limit. The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). Every child here is 8 to 11, so the rule covers all of them. The adults can't all go off to prepare another room and leave the children alone. At least one responsible adult needs to stay with the children throughout. The rules don't set an adult-to-child ratio, so "adult supervision" is the only standard.

Two related rules:
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). That duty is separate from supervising the children. It's unclear whether one adult can cover both, so the safest plan is to keep an adult with the children and have another at the entrance.
- The named hirer must stay on site for the whole event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).

**Other points**
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall's hours are 09:00 to 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3). Setup and clearing must fall within the booked hours.
- None of the committee changes in hall-changes.md alter these rules for that date.
- The quick guide is only a summary, and the governing rules above take precedence over it.
