# Q6, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-quick-guide.md
5. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

- **Portable speaker:** £10 per booking (hall-fees.md §4.2), so £10 for one.
- **Folding display boards:** £5 per set per booking (§4.3), so 3 sets come to £15.

The charges in hall-fees.md apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025 (§1.2), so a 6 September 2025 event is covered. None of the later committee changes in hall-changes.md touch equipment fees. The group discount doesn't apply to equipment either (§3.4).

Equipment can only be hired alongside a room, which the Meeting Room booking satisfies (§4.4). Availability still has to be confirmed under hall-bookings.md (§4.5).
