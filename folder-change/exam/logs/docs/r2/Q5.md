# Q5, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Fenwray
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-fees-archive.md
5. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

The correct ticket price for the child is **£3**.

- Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children aged under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That rule has applied since 1 March 2025. The child is 9 and the parent accompanies them throughout, so it covers this case.
- The volunteer's £2 price comes from the old schedule in `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025 and was superseded in full on 1 March 2025. The archive says it must not be used for current charges.
- The committee changes register has no amendment to the child ticket price. Its only ticket change is to the member price, from 1 February 2026, and that doesn't affect this case.

The 16 August 2025 screening date falls after the March 2025 change, so the £3 price applies.
