# Q7, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Quillbrook
3. Grep "amplif|22:00|21:45|curfew|music" in reference/hall
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Read reference/hall/hall-conduct.md

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is not allowed.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, allows amplified music until 22:00 on Fridays and Saturdays. That is the rule the hirer is relying on.
- The committee changes register replaces that section. Under `hall-changes.md` section 5, the committee decided on 20 March 2026 that Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30. It took effect on 1 April 2026.
- The controlling date for that change is the date the music is played. That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after 1 April, so the 21:30 limit applies.

The band's contract is a private arrangement between the band and the hirer. It can't override the hall's rules, so the band would need to finish 15 minutes earlier than the contract says.

Section 4.2 and the other conduct rules are unchanged. The quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md`) also gives 21:30.
