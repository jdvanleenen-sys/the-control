## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`), for a 19:30 start on 18 November 2025:

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40**. Section 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**. Section 3.3 says they open 30 minutes before the advertised start.

The changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to either rule. Its only change to this file is the audience cap in section 3.1, which doesn't affect these times.

A late arrival after 19:40 isn't guaranteed entry. Section 3.5 lets late arrivals in only during a suitable break.
