> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **The fee depends on when payment is received.** Membership fee changes follow the date the treasurer receives payment (`film-changes.md` §1.3, and `film-membership.md` §3.1 on when membership begins).
- **First person (30 June 2025):** This is before 1 July 2025, so the old fee applies (`film-changes.md` §1.4). That is £24 under `film-fees.md` §1.1.
- **Second person (2 July 2025):** The committee decision of 16 June 2025 raised annual adult membership to £28 from 1 July 2025 (`film-changes.md` §1.2). That decision replaced §1.1.

The fee isn't reduced for joining part-way through the year (`film-fees.md` §1.3). No joining or administration fee is added (§1.5).
