> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member price. They pay the standard admission of £7.**

- **Suspension:** Membership rule 5.4 (`film-membership.md`) says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. The membership is still active, but the member price is unavailable during the suspension.
- **Price:** Fees rule 2.2 gives the member price to "members with active membership". Because the suspension blocks that price, the standard rate in rule 2.1 applies: **£7 per screening**.
- **Member price on that date:** It would have been £4. The committee's change to £5 only applies to screenings from 1 February 2026 (`film-changes.md`, section 3), so it doesn't affect this date.
- **Age:** The member is 35, so no child or youth concession applies. Those cover under-12s (£3) and ages 12–15 (£5).

I didn't read `film-conduct.md` section 4, which covers how suspensions are imposed. The answer doesn't depend on it.
