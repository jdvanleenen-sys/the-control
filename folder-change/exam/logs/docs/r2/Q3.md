# Q3, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Answer

**No. The duty volunteer doesn't have to warn the visitor first.**

Under section 2.3 of the Conduct and Complaints rules (`film-conduct.md`), "Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning." Section 1.2 prohibits threats. The rules were effective from 1 April 2025, so they applied on 14 November 2025. None of the later committee amendments changes the conduct rules.

- **Warnings are for lesser conduct.** Sections 2.1 and 2.2 cover ordinary disruption. There the volunteer asks the person to stop, and can require them to leave only if they refuse. A serious threat skips that step.
- **Removal is discretionary.** Section 2.3 says "may", so the volunteer can remove the visitor immediately but isn't required to.
- **Record the incident.** The volunteer must record it within 48 hours (section 2.4), which would be by 16 November 2025.
- **Refunds.** Removal doesn't automatically give the visitor a refund right (section 5.3). Eligibility is decided under `film-refunds.md`.
