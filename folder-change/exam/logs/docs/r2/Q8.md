## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**No, the group doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Under `hall-fees.md` §3.2, a registered village group needs at least ten local members. Being registered locally isn't enough. This group has nine, so the 20% discount under §3.1 doesn't apply. The 25% rate in `hall-changes.md` doesn't apply either. It only takes effect on 1 February 2026, and this event is in October 2025.
- **Charge:** The Main Hall rate is £24 an hour (§2.1). The £28 rate in `hall-changes.md` only takes effect for events from 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply to a 15 October 2025 event. Two paid hours meets the two-hour minimum (§2.4), so the charge is 2 × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. Any kitchen or equipment charges and the £30 booking deposit would be separate. The deposit counts towards the total hire charge.
