# Q6, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Quillbrook
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Grep "speaker|board|equipment" in reference/hall

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

- Portable speaker: 1 × £10 = £10 (hall-fees.md §4.2)
- Folding display boards: 3 sets × £5 = £15 (hall-fees.md §4.3)

The current fee schedule applies because the booking starts after 1 April 2025 (§1.2). The older archive rates (£8 for the speaker, £4 per set of boards) don't apply. None of the committee changes touch equipment charges, and the group discount doesn't apply to equipment anyway (§3.4).

The Meeting Room is booked, so the equipment can be hired with it (§4.4). The hall must still confirm the equipment is available, and the hirer must request it when booking (hall-bookings.md §5.3).
