# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance is permitted, but the children can't be left without adult supervision.**

**Attendance**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2). Capacity counts organisers, performers and children (4.4).
- 16 children plus 5 adults is 21 people, which is 3 under the limit.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).
- None of the committee changes in hall-changes.md alter these rules for 19 October 2025.

**Supervision**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. The rules make no exception for short periods, such as while adults prepare another room.
- Some adults can go and prepare the other room, but enough adults must stay with the children throughout. The rules don't set an adult-to-child ratio, so "adequate" is for the hirer to judge.
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). The rules don't say whether that person can also be the one supervising the children, so I wouldn't assume they can.

**Other points that apply**
- The named hirer must remain on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3). The hirer is also responsible for guests (1.2).
- The hirer must explain the emergency exits to the organisers (hall-safety.md 1.3).
- The room used to prepare must be part of the booking. Setup must take place within booked hours (hall-bookings.md 3.5). Changes to rooms need written approval (6.1).
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall closes at 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3).
