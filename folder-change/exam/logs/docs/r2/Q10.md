# Q10, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
4. Grep "quorum|remote|video" in reference/hall

## Answer

**The rules don't say, so they don't settle whether the meeting is quorate.**

- **Quorum:** Rule 4.2 of the Trustee Committee Rules (`hall-trustees.md`) says "Four trustees form a quorum." With four trustees attending, the number is met only if the remote trustee counts.
- **Remote attendance:** Rule 6.3 says the rules "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely." Nothing in the rules allows remote attendance to count towards quorum, and nothing prevents it. I searched the other hall documents for "quorum", "remote" and "video" and found nothing else on the point.

Only three trustees are in the room. The meeting is quorate only if the remote trustee counts as present. The rules leave that open, so I can't say it would be.

Because the rules are silent, I'd suggest the following before 15 November:
1. Check whether the hall's governing document (constitution or charity model rules) covers remote participation. That is outside the files I have.
2. If it doesn't, have the committee pass a resolution at an earlier meeting to allow remote attendance and say whether it counts for quorum.
3. If neither is possible, get a fourth trustee into the room. That is the safest route, because decisions made at an inquorate meeting could be challenged.
