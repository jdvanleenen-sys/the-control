# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2).
- That limit counts organisers and children (4.4), so the headcount is 16 children + 5 adults = 21. That is 3 under the limit.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the event and any setup and clearing must fit within the 09:00–21:00 hours (hall-bookings.md 3.3, 3.5).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. The rules make no exception for short absences or for adults working in another room.
- At least one responsible adult must also remain near the main entrance (3.4). The adults can prepare the other room only if enough of them stay to supervise the children. Whoever is near the entrance must still meet the supervision requirement, unless a different adult is also with the children.
- The named hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3) and is responsible for guests (1.2).

**Other points:**
- The Committee Changes Register (hall-changes.md) doesn't alter any of these provisions.
- The other room is presumably a separate booking. If the two rooms are used together, the combined event is capped at 100 people in the building (hall-bookings.md 4.3).
- Today is 12 October 2026, so 19 October 2025 is in the past. I've answered on the rules as written, which were adopted on 12 February 2025.
