# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
4. Grep "quorum|remote|video|virtual" in reference/hall

## Answer

**The rules don't say whether a remote trustee counts towards quorum, so they don't settle whether the meeting would be quorate.**

- **Quorum:** Rule 4.2 says "Four trustees form a quorum." With all four attending, the number is met.
- **Remote attendance:** Rule 6.3 says the rules "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely." Nothing in 4.2 says quorum means trustees physically present, and nothing says video attendance counts. No other hall file mentions remote or video attendance.

If the video attendee counts, the meeting is quorate with exactly four. If they don't, only three are present and it is inquorate. In that case any decisions would be at risk of being invalid, including spending votes under 5.2.

Because the margin is exactly four, I'd treat the meeting as at risk. Before 15 November, you could:
- Ask the committee to agree, and record in the minutes, that video attendance counts for quorum.
- Have a fifth trustee attend in person.
- Move the remote trustee to in-person attendance.

I only checked the hall rules in this folder. If the hall has a constitution or governing document outside it, or if the hall is a registered charity, that document may cover remote attendance.
