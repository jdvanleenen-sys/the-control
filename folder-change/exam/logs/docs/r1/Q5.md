> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

**The child's ticket is £3.**

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` (effective 1 March 2025) says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. The child is 9 and the parent is with them throughout, so this applies.
- **The £2 price list is out of date:** £2 comes from section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 to 28 February 2025 and was superseded in full on 1 March 2025. The archive says it "must not be used for current charges."
- **No later change affects it:** The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) only changes the member admission price, adult membership, equipment, capacity and refund notice. None of those touch the under-12 price, so £3 still applies on 16 August 2025.
