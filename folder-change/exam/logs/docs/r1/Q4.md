## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.**

- **Suspension:** Membership rules 5.4 say a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. Their membership is still active, but the member price is blocked while they're suspended.
- **Standard price:** Fees rule 2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening.
- **No concession:** At 35, none of the child rates (2.3 and 2.4) apply.
- **Not the £5 rate:** The committee's 19 January 2026 decision raised the member price from £4 to £5, but only for screenings from 1 February 2026. It doesn't matter here, because the member price doesn't apply to this person anyway.

From 21 November 2025, once the suspension ends, they could claim the member price again. At a screening before 1 February 2026 that would be £4.
