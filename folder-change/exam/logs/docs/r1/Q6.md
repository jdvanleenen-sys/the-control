# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-quick-guide.md
5. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

Under hall-fees.md, section 4:
- **Portable speaker:** £10 per booking (4.2).
- **Folding display boards:** £5 per set per booking (4.3), so three sets come to £15.

That makes £10 + £15 = £25.

The committee changes register (hall-changes.md) doesn't amend any equipment fees. The charges apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025 (1.2), so a 6 September 2025 booking is covered. Equipment can only be hired alongside a room (4.4), which the Meeting Room booking satisfies. The 20% group discount doesn't apply to equipment either (3.4).

Availability of the speaker and boards still has to be confirmed under hall-bookings.md (4.5). I haven't checked that.
