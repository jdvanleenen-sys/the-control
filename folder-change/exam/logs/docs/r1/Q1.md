## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's Screening and Room Rules (`film-screenings.md`):

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40** on 18 November 2025. Section 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start, which here is 19:30.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**, 30 minutes before the advertised start (section 3.3).

The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to sections 3.3 or 3.4, so both rules apply unchanged on that date. A late arrival who holds a seat may still enter only during a suitable break (section 3.5).
