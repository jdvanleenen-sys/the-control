> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Base fee:** `film-fees.md` §1.1 sets annual adult membership at £24.
- **Fee change:** The committee's decision of 16 June 2025 (`film-changes.md` §1) raised the fee to £28 from 1 July 2025.
- **Which fee applies:** The date the treasurer receives payment decides it (§1.3). Membership also begins on that date (`film-membership.md` §3.1).
- **First person:** Their payment was received on 30 June 2025, before 1 July. They keep the old fee of £24 (§1.4).
- **Second person:** Their payment was received on 2 July 2025, after the change. They pay £28.

The date the applications were accepted doesn't matter. Payment is only due after acceptance (`film-membership.md` §2.5), and the fee follows the payment date.
