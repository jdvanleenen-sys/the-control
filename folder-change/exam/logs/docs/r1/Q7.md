# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

2. Glob **/*
3. Grep "amplif|22:00|21:45|curfew|music" in reference/hall
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is not allowed.**

- The original rule in `hall-conduct.md` §4.1 set the Friday and Saturday cutoff at 22:00. That is the rule the hirer is relying on.
- The Committee Changes Register (`hall-changes.md` §5) replaced §4.1 by a decision made on 20 March 2026. It took effect on 1 April 2026, and from then on Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played. That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after 1 April, so the new 21:30 cutoff applies. The date the band was hired or the contract was signed makes no difference.
- The quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md` §5) also gives 21:30.

The band's contract runs 15 minutes past the cutoff. The band needs to finish by 21:30, or the hirer should ask the committee for an exception. The other music rules in §4 still apply, including closed doors and windows and no external speakers.
