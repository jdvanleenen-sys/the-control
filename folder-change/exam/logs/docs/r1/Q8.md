## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**It doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Section 3.2 of `hall-fees.md` says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. This group has nine, so it falls short. Being registered locally isn't enough. The committee changes register only changes the discount percentage (section 3.1, to 25% from 1 February 2026). It leaves the ten-member minimum alone, and that change doesn't apply to an October 2025 event anyway.
- **Charge:** The event is on 15 October 2025, which is before the £28 rate takes effect on 1 November 2025. So the original Main Hall rate of £24 per hour applies (section 2.1). Two paid hours meets the two-hour minimum (section 2.4), so the charge is 2 × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit would also be required, and it counts towards the total (section 5.4). Kitchen or equipment charges would be extra.
