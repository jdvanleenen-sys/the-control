# Q2, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

- **First person (payment received 30 June 2025):** The fee is £24. From 1 July 2025 the fee is £28, but payments received before that date keep the old fee (film-changes.md, section 1.2 and 1.4).
- **Second person (payment received 2 July 2025):** The fee is £28. The date the payment is received sets the fee (film-changes.md, section 1.3).

Without the change, both would pay £24 (film-fees.md, section 1.1). The change replaces that section.

A payment counts as received when cash is handed over or a transfer clears (film-fees.md, section 4.5). I took the stated dates as those dates. If either payment was a bank transfer, the date that matters is when it cleared.
