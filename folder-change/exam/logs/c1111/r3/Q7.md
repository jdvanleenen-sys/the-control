# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-conduct.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Amplified music on a Friday or Saturday must stop by 21:30, because the controlling date is the date the music is played, 4 April 2026. That is after the 1 April 2026 effective date (hall-changes.md, section 5, replacing hall-conduct.md section 4.1). The band's 21:45 finish is 15 minutes too late.

The original 22:00 cutoff in hall-conduct.md section 4.1 would otherwise have allowed it. The 22:00 figure the hirer cites no longer applies to this date.
