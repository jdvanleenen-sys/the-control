# Q4, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the member price. The standard admission of £7 applies.

- **Why no discount:** A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, section 5.4). The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. Their membership is still active, but 5.4 removes the member price while it is suspended.
- **Price that applies:** Standard admission costs £7 per screening (film-fees.md, section 2.1).
- **What would otherwise apply:** An active member would pay £4 under film-fees.md section 2.2. The £5 member price in film-changes.md section 3 starts on 1 February 2026, so it doesn't apply to a screening on 8 November 2025. The difference is £3.
