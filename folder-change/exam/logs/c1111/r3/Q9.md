# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|supervis|children|child|under 1" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

Attendance is permitted, and the children may not be left without adult supervision.

**Attendance:** 21 people (16 children and 5 adults) is within the Meeting Room maximum of 24. Capacity counts organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). No changes entry replaces these sections.

**Supervision:** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. The rules do not make an exception for adults preparing another room. Leaving the children unsupervised would not meet section 3.3.

The rules do not say how many adults are needed per child. They also do not say whether supervision means an adult in the same room. I have not assumed either. Separately, at least one responsible adult must remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md, section 3.4).
