# Q8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, the group doesn't qualify. The room-hire charge without the discount is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine, so being registered locally isn't enough.
- **Charge:** Main Hall hire is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1), and two hours is the minimum paid booking (section 2.4). That gives 2 × £24 = £48.
- **Changes register:** The £28 rate in hall-changes.md section 2 takes effect on 1 November 2025, and the controlling date is the first date of the event. 15 October 2025 is earlier, so £24 still applies.
