# Q6, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is £25.

Under hall-fees.md section 4.2, a portable speaker costs £10 per booking. Under section 4.3, folding display boards cost £5 per set per booking, so three sets cost £15. That gives £10 + £15 = £25.

No entry in hall-changes.md replaces section 4.2 or 4.3. The register only replaces hall-fees.md sections 2.1 and 3.1 on fees, so the equipment charges stand as written.

The booking date of 6 September 2025 is after 1 April 2025, so these charges apply (hall-fees.md, section 1.2).
