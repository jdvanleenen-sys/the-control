# Q5, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "child|under 1|£2" in reference/film
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

The child pays £3. Children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult (film-fees.md, section 2.3). The parent accompanied the child throughout, and no entry in film-changes.md replaces section 2.3.

The £2 price comes from the superseded schedule in film-fees-archive.md, which doesn't govern.
