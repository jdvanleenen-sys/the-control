# Q4, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Grep "suspen" in reference/film

## Answer

No. A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, 5.4), and 8 November 2025 falls inside the 1–20 November suspension. They pay standard admission of **£7** per screening (film-fees.md, 2.1).

The member price in film-fees.md 2.2 is £4. The changes document raises it to £5 only from 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, 3.2), so it does not affect an 8 November 2025 screening. The discount is unavailable either way, and the difference from the standard price would have been £3.
