# Q8, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, it doesn't qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). Being registered locally isn't enough.

Without the discount, the room-hire charge is £48: two hours at £24 per hour. The 15 October 2025 event date is before the £28 rate takes effect on 1 November 2025, so the original rate applies (hall-fees.md, section 2.1; hall-changes.md, section 2).

The 25% rate in hall-changes.md section 3 starts on 1 February 2026, so it wouldn't apply to this date. Nor would it change the ten-member requirement.
