# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|supervis|capacity|children|under 1" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance:** Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 people, and capacities include organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, 4.2 and 4.4). The event has 16 children and 5 adults, which is 21 people.

**Leaving the children unsupervised:** No. All the children are 8 to 11, so they are under 12 and must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, 3.3). The rules don't allow them to be left without it while the adults prepare another room.
