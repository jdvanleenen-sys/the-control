# Q6, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is £25.

Each item is charged per booking: one portable speaker at £10 (hall-fees.md, 4.2) and three sets of folding display boards at £5 per set, which is £15 (hall-fees.md, 4.3). That gives £10 + £15 = £25.

The charges apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025 (hall-fees.md, 1.2), so a 6 September 2025 booking is covered. No entry in hall-changes.md replaces the equipment sections (4.2 and 4.3).
