# Q2, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

- **First person (payment received 30 June 2025):** £24. Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (film-changes.md, section 1.4).
- **Second person (payment received 2 July 2025):** £28. From 1 July 2025 annual adult membership costs £28 (film-changes.md, section 1.2). The date the payment is received sets the fee (film-changes.md, section 1.3).

Without the change, both would pay £24 under film-fees.md, section 1.1. That section is replaced from 1 July 2025.
