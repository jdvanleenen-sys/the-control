# Q10, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep "quorum|trustee|remote|video" in reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either question.

- **Remote trustee and quorum:** The rules state that they "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely" (hall-trustees.md, 6.3). Nothing else in them says whether a trustee on a video call is "present" for quorum.
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, 4.2). This turns on the fact the rules leave open.
  - If a remote trustee counts, there are four present and the meeting is quorate.
  - If a remote trustee does not count, there are three present and it is not quorate.

The hall-changes.md document has nothing on quorum or remote attendance, so it doesn't alter this.
