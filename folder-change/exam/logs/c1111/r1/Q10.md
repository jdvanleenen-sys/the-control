## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep "quorum|trustee|remote|video" in reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either question.

- **Remote trustee counting towards quorum:** The rules state that they "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely" (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). They don't say whether a video-call attendee is "present" for quorum. I found nothing on remote attendance in hall-changes.md either.
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). The answer depends on whether the remote trustee counts.
  - If the remote trustee counts, there are four present, which meets the quorum.
  - If the remote trustee doesn't count, only three are present, which is one short.

The documents don't settle which of these applies. hall-changes.md has no entry replacing section 4.2 or section 6.3.
