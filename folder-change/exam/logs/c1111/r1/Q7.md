# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "music|amplified" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. The music is played on 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April 2026 effective date. The cutoff for Saturday amplified music is therefore 21:30, so the band cannot play until 21:45 (hall-changes.md, section 5).

The hirer's 22:00 figure comes from hall-conduct.md section 4.1, which section 5 of hall-changes.md replaces. The controlling date is the date the music is played (section 5.3). The band would be 15 minutes over the 21:30 cutoff.
