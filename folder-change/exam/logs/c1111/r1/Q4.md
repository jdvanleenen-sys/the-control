# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the member price. The standard admission price of £7 applies.

- **Why no discount:** A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, 5.4). The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. Their membership is still active, but the suspension rule bars the discount. Suspension of up to 30 calendar days is allowed (film-conduct.md, 4.1).
- **What applies instead:** Standard admission costs £7 per screening (film-fees.md, 2.1).
- **What would otherwise apply:** The member price on 8 November 2025 would be £4 (film-fees.md, 2.2). The later £5 member price (film-changes.md, 3.2) starts on 1 February 2026, so it doesn't apply to this date. Without the suspension they would have paid £4, which is £3 less than the £7 charged.
