# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is £25.

Under hall-fees.md, section 4.2, a portable speaker is £10 per booking. Under section 4.3, folding display boards are £5 per set per booking, so three sets cost £15. That gives £10 + £15 = £25.

No changes entry replaces sections 4.2 or 4.3. The register only replaces 2.1 and 3.1 of hall-fees.md. The 6 September 2025 booking is also after the 1 April 2025 start date in section 1.2.
