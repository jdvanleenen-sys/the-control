# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep "conduct|warning|remov|leave|threat" in reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. For a serious threat, the duty volunteer may remove the visitor immediately without a warning (film-conduct.md, section 2.3).

Otherwise, under sections 2.1–2.2, a person is asked to stop and may be required to leave only if they refuse. That request-first sequence doesn't apply to a serious threat.

film-changes.md has no entry that changes the conduct sections.
