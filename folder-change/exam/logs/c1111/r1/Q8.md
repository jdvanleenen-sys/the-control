# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, it doesn't qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). Being registered locally isn't enough on its own.

Without the discount, the room-hire charge is £48: two paid hours at £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, sections 2.1 and 2.4). The £28 rate in hall-changes.md section 2 applies only to events starting on or after 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply to an event on 15 October 2025. The 25% discount in hall-changes.md section 3 doesn't apply either. It applies only from 1 February 2026, and it has the same ten-member requirement.
