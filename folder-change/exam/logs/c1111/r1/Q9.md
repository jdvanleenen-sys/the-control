# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|supervis|capacity|children|under 1" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted. Unsupervised children: no.**

**Attendance.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that count includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). The event has 16 children and 5 adults, which is 21 people, so it is within the limit. No changes-document entry replaces these sections.

**Supervision.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. They may not be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. Some adults would have to stay with the children.

The documents don't say how many adults supervision requires or what counts as supervision. Section 3.3 only requires that the children stay under adult supervision.
