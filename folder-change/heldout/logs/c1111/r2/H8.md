# H8, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: On 12 October 2025, an organiser wants to use both the Main Hall and Meeting Room for one event. The expected attendance is 89 people in the Main Hall and 23 in the Meeting Room, including organisers and children. Both rooms would individually be within their stated capacities. Is the combined event permitted at that attendance, and by how many people would it exceed any applicable limit?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "capacity|combined|both rooms|Meeting Room" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

No, the combined event is not permitted. It would be 112 people in the building, which is 12 over the 100-person limit for combined events (hall-bookings.md, section 4.3).

The 89 in the Main Hall and 23 in the Meeting Room are each within their own limits of 90 and 24 (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.1 and 4.2). Those limits would otherwise apply on their own. Section 4.3 sets the building-wide cap for combined events, and section 4.4 counts organisers, performers and children toward it.

hall-changes.md does not replace section 4, so the 12 October 2025 date makes no difference to this answer.
