# H1, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: On 12 May 2025, a volunteer takes a £9 cash payment for a screening ticket. The customer asks whether the treasurer is required to issue a receipt. Is a receipt compulsory, and what is the payment threshold?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "receipt" in reference/film
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A receipt is not compulsory for this payment. The treasurer issues a receipt only for payments of £10 or more, and the threshold is £10 (film-fees.md, section 4.4). The £9 payment is below that threshold.
