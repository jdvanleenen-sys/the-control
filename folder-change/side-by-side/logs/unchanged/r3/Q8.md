# Q8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The group does not qualify, and the room-hire charge without the discount is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine, so registration alone is not enough. The 15 October 2025 event date is before the 1 February 2026 change to the discount percentage, so that change doesn't apply. It doesn't affect eligibility anyway.
- **Charge:** The Main Hall rate is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1). The £28 rate in hall-changes.md, section 2 takes effect only for events on or after 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply to 15 October 2025. Two paid hours at £24 is £48.
