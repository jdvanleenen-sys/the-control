# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
2. Grep "^#|music|amplified" in reference/hall/hall-conduct.md

## Answer

No. The music is played on 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April 2026 effective date, so the changes register governs. Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is too late (hall-changes.md, section 5, replacing hall-conduct.md section 4.1). The hirer's 22:00 cutoff is the old wording in hall-conduct.md section 4.1, and it no longer applies.
