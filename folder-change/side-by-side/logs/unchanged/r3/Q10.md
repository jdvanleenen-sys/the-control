## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The documents don't say whether a remote trustee counts towards the quorum, so I can't give a yes or no on either question.

- **Quorum:** Four trustees form a quorum (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). The rules say nothing about whether a trustee must be physically present to count.
- **Remote attendance:** The rules "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely" (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). The changes register doesn't alter this.

The answer turns on whether the video-call trustee counts as present, which the documents leave open:
- **If they count as present:** four trustees would meet the quorum of four.
- **If they don't count:** only three would be present, which is below the quorum.
