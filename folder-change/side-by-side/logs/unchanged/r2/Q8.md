# Q8, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The group does not qualify, and the room-hire charge without the discount is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine, so it falls short. Being registered locally is not enough on its own. The changes register replaces section 3.1 (the percentage) only from 1 February 2026, and it leaves section 3.2 unchanged (hall-changes.md, section 3).
- **Charge:** The event is on 15 October 2025, before the £28 rate takes effect on 1 November 2025. The £24 per hour rate therefore applies (hall-fees.md, section 2.1; hall-changes.md, section 2). Two paid hours at £24 is £48, which meets the two-hour minimum (hall-fees.md, section 2.4).
