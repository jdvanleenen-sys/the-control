# Q6, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is £25. That is £10 for the portable speaker plus £15 for three sets of folding display boards.

- Portable speaker: £10 per booking (hall-fees.md, 4.2).
- Folding display boards: £5 per set per booking, so 3 × £5 = £15 (hall-fees.md, 4.3).

No changes entry replaces the equipment charges.
