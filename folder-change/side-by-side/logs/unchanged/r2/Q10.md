# Q10, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep "quorum|remote|video|trustee" in reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either question.

- **Remote trustee and quorum:** The rules don't specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, 6.3). They also don't say whether a remote attendee counts towards quorum. The changes document has nothing on the point either (hall-changes.md).
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, 4.2). Three in person is below that. The meeting is quorate only if the video attendee counts, and the documents leave that undecided.
