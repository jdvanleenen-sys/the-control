# Q4, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the member price. A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, 5.4). Their suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so it covers the screening on 8 November 2025.

The standard admission price of £7 applies (film-fees.md, 2.1). The £4 member rate in film-fees.md 2.2 is for members with active membership. The January 2026 change to £5 doesn't apply because it only takes effect from 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, 3.2).
