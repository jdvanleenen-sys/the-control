# Q3, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep "conduct|warning|remove|leave" in reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A serious threat may lead to immediate removal without a warning (film-conduct.md, section 2.3). A warning is only part of the ordinary route for disruptive behaviour, where the person is asked to stop and may be required to leave if they refuse (section 2.1–2.2). The visitor's lack of a previous warning doesn't change this.

The changes document (film-changes.md) doesn't replace any conduct section, so section 2.3 governs for 14 November 2025.
