# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
2. Grep "4\\.1|music|amplified" in reference/hall/hall-conduct.md

## Answer

No. The music is played on 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April 2026 effective date. The changes register therefore replaces the 22:00 cutoff, and Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30 (hall-changes.md, section 5, items 5.1–5.3; it replaces hall-conduct.md, section 4.1). Playing until 21:45 would go past that.
