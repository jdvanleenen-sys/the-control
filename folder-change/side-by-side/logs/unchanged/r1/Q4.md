# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.

- A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, s5.4). The suspension covers 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. This is within the 30-day limit in film-conduct.md s4.1.
- Standard admission costs £7 per screening (film-fees.md, s2.1).
- Their age doesn't change the price. Neither the under-12 rate (£3, s2.3) nor the 12–15 rate (£5, s2.4) applies to a 35-year-old.
- The member rate was £4 on that date (film-fees.md, s2.2). The change to £5 starts on 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, s3), so it doesn't apply to a screening on 8 November 2025.
