# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

It does not qualify, and the room-hire charge without the discount is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine, so it falls short. Being registered locally isn't enough on its own. The 15 October 2025 event date is before the 1 February 2026 effective date of the changes register's 25% replacement of section 3.1 (hall-changes.md, section 3). That replacement therefore doesn't apply, and it doesn't change the ten-member requirement in any case.
- **Charge:** The Main Hall rate for an event on 15 October 2025 is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1). The £28 rate takes effect only for events from 1 November 2025 (hall-changes.md, section 2). Two paid hours at £24 come to £48, and two hours is also the minimum paid booking (hall-fees.md, section 2.4).
