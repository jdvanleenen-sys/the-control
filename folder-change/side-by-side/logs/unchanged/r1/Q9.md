# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|supervis|children|under 1" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance:** Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 people, counting organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). 16 children plus 5 adults is 21.

**Leaving the children unsupervised:** No. Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule applies to them. The documents set no exception for adults preparing another room.

The changes register (hall-changes.md) doesn't replace any of these sections.
