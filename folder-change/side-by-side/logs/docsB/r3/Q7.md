# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is 15 minutes too late.**

- **Original rule:** `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1 set the Friday and Saturday amplified music cutoff at 22:00. The hirer is relying on that wording.
- **Replacement:** The committee decided on 20 March 2026 to replace section 4.1 (`hall-changes.md`, section 5). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- **Which date applies:** The controlling date is the date the music is played. That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies. The date the contract was signed doesn't matter.

The hall's quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md`) also gives 21:30. The band's contract can't override the hall rules, so the band would need to end its set by 21:30 or the hirer would need to amend the contract.
