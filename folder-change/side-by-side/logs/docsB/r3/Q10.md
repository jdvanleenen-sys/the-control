## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Answer

**The rules don't say, so I can't confirm that this meeting would be quorate.**

- **Quorum:** Rule 4.2 says "Four trustees form a quorum." Exactly four trustees are available.
- **Remote attendance:** Rule 6.3 says the rules "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely." Nothing in them allows or forbids a video-call trustee counting towards quorum. I also searched `hall-changes.md` for anything on quorum, remote or video attendance, and found nothing.

The three trustees in person are one short of quorum. The meeting is quorate only if the video attendee counts, and the rules give no answer on that.

Because the rules are silent, I wouldn't treat the meeting as quorate without more authority. If the meeting isn't quorate, any decisions it makes could be open to challenge. Some options:

1. Have the fourth trustee attend in person, which removes the doubt.
2. Before 15 November, have the committee agree and minute a decision to allow remote attendance to count. Check first whether the rules let the committee settle this itself. They don't say.
3. Check whether the hall has a separate constitution or governing document that covers remote attendance. I only had these rules to go on.

Whichever option you choose, record the reasoning in the minutes. Rule 4.5 requires the next meeting to approve them.
