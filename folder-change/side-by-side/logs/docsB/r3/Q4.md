## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.**

- **Suspension:** Under `film-membership.md` §5.4, a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The membership is still active, but the suspension runs from 1 to 20 November 2025. That covers the screening on 8 November. The suspension is 20 days, which is within the 30-day maximum in `film-conduct.md` §4.1.
- **Price:** `film-fees.md` §2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening. Their age of 35 doesn't qualify them for any child rate (§2.3–2.4).
- **Amendments:** The committee later raised the member price from £4 to £5 (`film-changes.md` §3). That applies only to screenings from 1 February 2026, so it doesn't affect an 8 November 2025 screening. Because they're suspended, the member price doesn't apply to them either way.

The suspension doesn't bar them from attending. Membership doesn't guarantee admission (`film-membership.md` §4.4), and entry remains subject to the capacity rules. They can still buy a £7 ticket, subject to capacity.
