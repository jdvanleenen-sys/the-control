## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

4. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`):

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40** on 18 November 2025. Section 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**, 30 minutes before the advertised start (section 3.3).

The committee changes register doesn't amend either of these sections. Its only amendment to this file is the maximum audience in 3.1, which doesn't affect your question.

If the person arrives after the film has started, section 3.5 lets them in only during a suitable break.
