> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- The fee depends on the date the treasurer receives the payment (changes register, 1.3). Under film-fees.md 4.5, a payment counts as received when cash is handed over or a transfer clears.
- The original adult fee was £24 (film-fees.md 1.1). The committee's decision of 16 June 2025 raised it to £28 from 1 July 2025. Payments received before that date keep the old fee (changes register, 1.2 and 1.4).
- The first payment arrived on 30 June 2025, before the change, so the fee is £24. The second arrived on 2 July 2025, after it, so the fee is £28.
- The date of acceptance doesn't affect the fee. Payment is only due after acceptance (film-membership.md 2.5), and the fee is set by when payment is received.
- Joining partway through the year doesn't reduce the fee (film-fees.md 1.3).

The £24 payment you described for the first person matches the correct fee. The second person owes £28, not £24.
