# Q8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**The group doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Under `hall-fees.md` §3.1–3.2, a registered village group gets the discount only if it has at least ten local members. Being registered locally isn't enough, and this group has nine, so it misses by one. The discount is refused.
- **Charge:** The Main Hall rate on 15 October 2025 is £24 per hour (`hall-fees.md` §2.1). The rise to £28 per hour in `hall-changes.md` §2 takes effect on 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply. The booking is for two paid hours, which meets the two-hour minimum in §2.4. That gives 2 × £24 = **£48**.

The £48 covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit (§5.1) counts towards it (§5.4). The 15 October date is also before the 25% discount takes effect on 1 February 2026, but that doesn't matter here, because the group fails the member threshold.
