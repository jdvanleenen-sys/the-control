## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

The child's ticket costs **£3**.

- **Current rule:** `film-fees.md` section 2.3 says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That document took effect on 1 March 2025, so it covers a screening on 16 August 2025. A 9-year-old with a parent for the whole screening meets both conditions.
- **The £2 price list is out of date:** The volunteer's figure matches section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025, and the archive says it "must not be used for current charges."
- **No amendment changes it:** `film-changes.md` doesn't touch section 2.3. Its only ticket-price change is the member price, from 1 February 2026, and that doesn't apply to a child's ticket.
