# Q6, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*

4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Grep "speaker|display board|equipment" in reference/hall

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

- One portable speaker costs £10 per booking (`hall-fees.md` §4.2).
- Three sets of folding display boards cost 3 × £5 = £15 (`hall-fees.md` §4.3).

A 6 September 2025 booking falls under the current fees, which apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025 (§1.2). The older rates in `hall-fees-archive.md` (£8 and £4) don't apply. None of the committee changes in `hall-changes.md` touch equipment charges. The 20% village-group discount wouldn't reduce this total either, because it excludes equipment (§3.4).

The charge assumes the equipment is available. The hirer must request it when booking (`hall-bookings.md` §5.3), and availability has to be confirmed (`hall-fees.md` §4.5).
