# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2), and that figure includes organisers and children (4.4).
- 16 children plus 5 adults is 21 people, which is 3 under the limit.
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the event must finish by 21:00 (3.3, 3.4). No later committee change affects Sunday hours.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2). Fire safety requirements also apply (hall-bookings.md 4.5).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All the children are 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them.
- The rules set no adult-to-child ratio and don't say what counts as supervision. They only say the children must not be left without adult supervision.
- With five adults, some could prepare the other room while at least one stays with the children.
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). The rules don't say whether one adult can cover both duties, so I wouldn't assume they can.
- The named hirer is responsible for guests (hall-conduct.md 1.2) and must stay on site throughout (1.3).

**Preparing another room.** Setup must fall within booked hours (hall-bookings.md 3.5). The other room would need to be booked too, and the Meeting Room booking doesn't cover it. Serious breaches can lead to the event being ended, with no refund of room hire (hall-conduct.md 6.1, 6.2).
