# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Answer

**No.** Under section 2.3 of the conduct rules (`film-conduct.md`), "Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning." The duty volunteer can require the visitor to leave straight away, even though the visitor has had no earlier warning.

- **Rules in force:** The conduct rules took effect on 1 April 2025, so they applied on 14 November 2025. The changes register has no amendments to the conduct rules.
- **Ordinary disruption:** The usual route in sections 2.1 and 2.2 is different. The volunteer asks the person to stop, and only someone who refuses may be required to leave. Section 2.3 lets the volunteer skip that step for serious threats.
- **Wording:** Section 2.3 says "may", so immediate removal is allowed but not compulsory.
- **Afterwards:** The volunteer must record the incident within 48 hours (2.4). Removal does not by itself end any society membership (2.5). It also does not automatically give the visitor a refund right (5.3).
