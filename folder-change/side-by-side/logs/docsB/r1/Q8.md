# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Under `hall-fees.md` §3.1–3.2, a registered village group needs at least ten local members to get the discount. Being registered locally isn't enough, and nine members is one short.
- **Charge:** The booking is for 15 October 2025. The committee's rise in the Main Hall rate to £28 only takes effect for events on or after 1 November 2025 (`hall-changes.md` §2). The original £24 per hour in §2.1 therefore applies. Two paid hours meets the two-hour minimum (§2.4), so the charge is 2 × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. A kitchen, equipment or the £30 booking deposit would be separate (§2.3, §4, §5). The booking deposit counts towards the total hire charge (§5.4).
