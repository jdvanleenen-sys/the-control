## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

- Portable speaker: 1 × £10 = £10 (`hall-fees.md` §4.2)
- Folding display boards: 3 sets × £5 = £15 (§4.3)

The fees apply because the booking starts after 1 April 2025 (§1.2). The changes register doesn't alter any equipment charges. I didn't check `hall-bookings.md`, so I haven't confirmed the equipment is available for the Meeting Room on that date (§4.5). That doesn't change the charge.
