## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.**

- **Suspension:** Membership rule 5.4 (`film-membership.md`) says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs from 1 to 20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. Their membership is still active, but they can't claim the member price.
- **Member price:** Fee rule 2.2 (`film-fees.md`) gives active members £4. This question doesn't depend on it. The committee's change to £5 only applies to screenings from 1 February 2026, so it doesn't affect an 8 November 2025 screening.
- **Standard price:** Fee rule 2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening. The child rates in 2.3 and 2.4 are for under-16s, and this member is 35, so they don't apply.

If the member wants to attend, they pay £7. Admission is also subject to the capacity limit (42 at that date).
