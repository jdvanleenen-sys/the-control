# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (6)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Fenwray
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-fees-archive.md
6. Grep "child|under 12|accompan" in reference/film

## Answer

**The correct ticket price is £3.**

- The current rule is section 2.3 of `film-fees.md`. It says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. It was adopted on 12 February 2025 and took effect on 1 March 2025.
- The child is 9 and the parent is with them throughout, so they meet both conditions. The screening on 16 August 2025 is after the rule took effect.
- The volunteer's £2 price comes from `film-fees-archive.md`, section 2.3. That schedule ran only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025 and is marked "must not be used for current charges."
- The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to child pricing. Its only ticket change is the member price, which rose from £4 to £5 from 1 February 2026. That doesn't apply here, because the child isn't a member and the screening was in 2025.
