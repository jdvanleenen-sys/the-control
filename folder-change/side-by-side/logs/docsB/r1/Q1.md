## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Steps (5)

3. Read reference/film/film-screenings.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`):

- **Seat hold:** Reserved seats are held until **19:40** on 18 November 2025. That is 10 minutes after the advertised 19:30 start (section 3.4).
- **Doors:** Doors normally open 30 minutes before the advertised start, so at **19:00** (section 3.3).

The changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to either section. A latecomer who holds a reservation can enter only during a suitable break (section 3.5).
