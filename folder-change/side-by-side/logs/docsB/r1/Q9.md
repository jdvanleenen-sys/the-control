# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2).
- Capacity includes organisers and children (4.4).
- 16 children plus 5 adults is 21, so the event is 3 under the limit.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children alone: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All the children are aged 8 to 11, so every one of them is covered.
- The rules set no exception for short absences or for adults preparing another room. At least one adult must stay with the children the whole time.
- A separate rule requires at least one responsible adult to remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). The rules don't say whether one adult can do both jobs. With five adults, the safest plan is to keep one with the children and have one cover the entrance. Only the remaining adults should go to prepare the other room.
- The named hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).
- If the second room is also hired, its attendees count toward the 100-person building limit (hall-bookings.md 4.3).

**Date check:**
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday. Sunday hours are 09:00 to 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3), and setup and clearing must fall within the booked hours.
- None of the committee changes in hall-changes.md affect these provisions on that date. They are all effective from November 2025 or later, and none touch capacity or supervision.
