## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is not allowed.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, set a 22:00 cut-off for amplified music on Fridays and Saturdays. That is the rule the hirer is relying on.
- A committee decision of 20 March 2026 replaced section 4.1 (`hall-changes.md`, section 5). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The change turns on the date the music is played. That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April start date, so the 21:30 rule applies.
- The contract says 21:45, but it can't override the hall rules. The band would be 15 minutes over.

The hirer's 22:00 figure is the old, superseded wording. The hall's own quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md`) already shows 21:30. The band's set will need to end 15 minutes earlier than the contract says.
