## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- The fee depends on the date the treasurer receives payment (film-changes.md §1.3). Payment counts as received when cash is handed over or a transfer clears (film-fees.md §4.5).
- The committee decision of 16 June 2025 raised annual adult membership from £24 to £28 from 1 July 2025. Payments received before that date keep the old fee (film-changes.md §1.2 and §1.4).
- The first person's payment was received on 30 June 2025, before the change, so the fee is £24 (film-fees.md §1.1).
- The second person's payment was received on 2 July 2025, after the change, so the fee is £28.
- The fee isn't reduced for joining partway through the year (film-fees.md §1.3). No joining or administration fee is charged (§1.5).
