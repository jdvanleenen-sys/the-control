## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**The group doesn't qualify for the discount, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Section 3.2 of `hall-fees.md` says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. This group has nine, so being registered locally isn't enough. The discount is out whichever rate applies. The committee later raised it from 20% to 25%, but that change only starts on 1 February 2026, and this event is on 15 October 2025.
- **Rate:** The Main Hall costs £24 per hour under section 2.1. The committee's £28 rate (`hall-changes.md` section 2) only applies to events from 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply here.
- **Charge:** Two paid hours at £24 is £48. That meets the two-hour minimum in section 2.4.

The £48 covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit counts towards it. Kitchen and equipment are extra if requested. The booking desk's quick guide quotes £28 and 25%, but it's only a summary and says the governing rules and change register prevail.
