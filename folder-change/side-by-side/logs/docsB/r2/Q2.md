# Q2, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md
5. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Fee rule:** Film-fees.md §1.1 sets annual adult membership at £24. The committee decision of 16 June 2025 (film-changes.md §1) raised it to £28 from 1 July 2025.
- **Which fee applies:** The date the payment is received decides the fee (changes §1.3). Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (§1.4). Fees aren't reduced for joining partway through the year (fees §1.3), so there's no pro-rating.
- **First person:** The treasurer received their payment on 30 June 2025, before the change, so they pay £24.
- **Second person:** The treasurer received their payment on 2 July 2025, after the change, so they pay £28.

Under film-membership.md §3, each membership starts on the date the treasurer receives payment and runs to 28 February 2026.
