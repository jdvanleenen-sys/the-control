# Q6, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Grep "speaker|display board|equipment" in reference/hall
5. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

The 6 September 2025 booking falls after the 1 April 2025 start date for the current charges (`hall-fees.md` §1.2). So the current fees apply, not the lower rates in the archive file.

- One portable speaker is £10 per booking (§4.2), so £10.
- Three sets of folding display boards are £5 per set per booking (§4.3), so 3 × £5 = £15.

The group discount doesn't apply to equipment (§3.4), so it can't reduce this. Equipment can only be hired alongside a room (§4.4), and the Meeting Room booking meets that. Under `hall-bookings.md` §5.3, the hirer must request the equipment when making the booking.
