# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (`hall-bookings.md` 4.2). That limit includes organisers, performers and children (4.4). Your event has 16 children and 5 adults, so 21 people, which is 3 under the limit. The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (`hall-safety.md` 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** `hall-safety.md` 3.3 says children under 12 must remain under adult supervision. All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers all of them. At least one adult must stay with them while the others prepare the other room. Some other points:
- **Main entrance:** at least one responsible adult must also remain near the main entrance (3.4). The rules don't say whether that can be the same adult who supervises the children. If the supervising adult can't cover the entrance from where they are, you need a second adult there.
- **Hirer on site:** the named hirer must remain on site throughout the event (`hall-conduct.md` 1.3).
- **Setup times:** setting up the other room has to happen within booked hours (`hall-bookings.md` 3.5).

**Other points**
- **Changes register:** `hall-changes.md` doesn't alter any of these provisions. Its entries cover fees, weekday hours, Friday and Saturday music, refunds and emergency spending.
- **Date:** 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall closes at 21:00 (`hall-bookings.md` 3.3). Today is 13 October 2026, so that date has already passed. If you meant 2026, the answers above are the same, because nothing in the changes register affects them.
