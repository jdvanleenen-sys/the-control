## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.**

- **Suspension blocks member prices.** Membership Rules 5.4 (`film-membership.md`) says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The membership stays active, but its rights are suspended. The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025 and is within the 30-day limit in Conduct 4.1. The screening on 8 November falls inside it.
- **Member price doesn't apply.** Fees 2.2 (`film-fees.md`) gives the member price only to "members with active membership." This member is suspended, so they can't claim it. The price was £4 on that date. The committee raised it to £5 only from 1 February 2026, and that change doesn't matter here.
- **Standard price applies.** Fees 2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening. Their age (35) doesn't qualify for the child rates in 2.3 or 2.4.

This assumes the committee followed the suspension procedure in Conduct 4.2–4.3, which requires a majority vote and a written decision stating the reason and dates.
