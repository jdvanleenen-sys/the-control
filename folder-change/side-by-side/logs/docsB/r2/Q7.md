# Q7, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

2. Grep Quillbrook
3. Grep "amplif|21:45|22:00|music" in reference/hall

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is too late.**

- The general rule the hirer cites is `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1. It set the Friday and Saturday cutoff at 22:00.
- The committee replaced that section in the changes register (`hall-changes.md`, section 5). The decision was made on 20 March 2026 and took effect on 1 April 2026. It says Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played. That is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the new 21:30 cutoff applies.
- The hirer is relying on the original 22:00 wording, which no longer governs. The band's contract can't override the hall rule either. The 21:45 time in it is 15 minutes past the cutoff.

The band should finish by 21:30. The contract time would need amending.
