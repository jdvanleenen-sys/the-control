# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|child|supervis|under 1" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that figure includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). Sixteen children plus five adults is 21.

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** All eight-to-eleven-year-olds are under 12, so they must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). The rules give no exception for adults preparing another room.
