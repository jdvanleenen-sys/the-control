# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify.** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine. Being registered locally isn't enough on its own.

**The room-hire charge is £48.** Main Hall hire is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1), and two paid hours is the minimum booking (section 2.4). The event is on 15 October 2025. The change to £28 per hour only takes effect for events from 1 November 2025 (hall-changes.md, section 2), so it doesn't apply here.

The discount change (hall-changes.md, section 3) takes effect on 1 February 2026, after this event. It wouldn't alter the answer either way, because the group is under the ten-member threshold.
