# Q3, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep "conduct|warning|leave|remov" in reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A serious threat may lead to immediate removal without a warning, so the duty volunteer does not have to warn the visitor first (film-conduct.md, section 2.3).

Without that rule, section 2.2 would apply. It lets a person be required to leave only if they refuse to stop disruptive behaviour after being asked under section 2.1. Section 2.3 removes that step for serious threats.

I checked film-changes.md, and it has no amendment to the conduct rules.
