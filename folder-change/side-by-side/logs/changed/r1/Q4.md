# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No. The member can't claim the member price, and they pay standard admission of £7.

- **Why no discount:** A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, 5.4). The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. The £4 rate is for members with active membership (film-fees.md, 2.2), and a suspended member can't claim it even though the membership is still active.
- **What applies instead:** Standard admission is £7 per screening (film-fees.md, 2.1).
- **Change check:** The committee decision of 19 January 2026 raises the member price to £5, but only from 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, 3.2–3.3). It doesn't apply to a 2025 screening, and the member price is not available to this person anyway.

I assumed the suspension is a valid one under film-conduct.md, section 4. The question doesn't say.
