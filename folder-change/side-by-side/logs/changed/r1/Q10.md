# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep "quorum|trustee|remote|video" in reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no. The rules do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). Nothing in the changes document (hall-changes.md) addresses quorum or remote attendance either.

Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). Whether a trustee on a video call counts towards those four is therefore undefined.

- **If a remote trustee counts as present:** four trustees (three in person plus one remote) meet the quorum, so the meeting would be quorate.
- **If a remote trustee does not count:** only three are present, which is below four, so the meeting would not be quorate.

The rules don't say which of these applies.
