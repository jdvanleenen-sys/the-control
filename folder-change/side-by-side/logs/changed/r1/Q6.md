# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**. Equipment charges are per booking, and no change replaces them.

- Portable speaker: £10 per booking (hall-fees.md, 4.2).
- Folding display boards: £5 per set per booking, so three sets come to £15 (hall-fees.md, 4.3).

The changes register replaces only hall-fees.md 2.1 and 3.1 in that file. It doesn't touch section 4, so the original charges apply to a 6 September 2025 booking (hall-changes.md, 1.1–1.2).
