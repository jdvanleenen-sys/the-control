# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "4\\.1|music|amplified" in reference/hall/hall-conduct.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Amplified music on a Friday or Saturday must stop by 21:30, so the band cannot play until 21:45.

- **Rule that decides it:** The Committee Changes Register replaces hall-conduct.md section 4.1 from 1 April 2026. The controlling date is the date the music is played, which is 4 April 2026, so the replacement applies (hall-changes.md, section 5).
- **What would otherwise apply:** Hall-conduct.md section 4.1 sets a 22:00 cutoff, which is the figure the hirer cites. The 21:45 end is 15 minutes past the current 21:30 cutoff. The old 22:00 cutoff would have allowed it.
