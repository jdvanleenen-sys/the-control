# Q7, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "4\\.1|music|amplified" in reference/hall/hall-conduct.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Amplified music on a Friday or Saturday must stop by 21:30, so the band may not play until 21:45.

The changes register replaces hall-conduct.md section 4.1 from 1 April 2026. The controlling date is the date the music is played (hall-changes.md, section 5). The band plays on 4 April 2026, which is after that date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies.

Without the change, hall-conduct.md section 4.1 would allow music until 22:00. The new cutoff is 30 minutes earlier, and 21:45 falls in that gap.
