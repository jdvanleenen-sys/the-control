# Q8, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify.** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine. Being registered locally isn't enough. The change of 15 January 2026 (hall-changes.md, section 3) only raises the discount percentage. It applies from 1 February 2026, so it doesn't reach a 15 October 2025 event anyway. It leaves the ten-member requirement unchanged.

**Room-hire charge without the discount: £48.** Main Hall hire is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1) for two paid hours. The £28 rate in hall-changes.md, section 2 takes effect on 1 November 2025, based on the first date of the event. It doesn't apply to 15 October 2025. Two hours is also the minimum paid booking (hall-fees.md, section 2.4).
