# Q4, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

**No.** A suspended member cannot claim member prices, so they aren't entitled to the member admission price. They pay standard admission of **£7**.

- **Suspension:** "A suspended member cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices" (film-membership.md, §5.4). The suspension covers 8 November 2025, and 30 days or fewer is within the committee's limit (film-conduct.md, §4.1).
- **Price that applies:** Standard admission is £7 per screening (film-fees.md, §2.1). They are an adult, so none of the child rates in §2.3–2.4 apply.
- **What would otherwise apply:** Without the suspension, they would pay the member price. The changes document replaces film-fees.md §2.2 (£4) with £5 only from 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, §3). For a screening on 8 November 2025, the member price would therefore have been £4 (film-fees.md, §2.2). The suspension makes the difference £3 (£7 instead of £4).
