# Q1, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-screenings.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

The society must hold the seat until 19:40, and doors normally open at 19:00.

- **Seat hold:** Reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start. With a 19:30 start, that is 19:40 (film-screenings.md, section 3.4).
- **Doors:** Doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start, which is 19:00 (film-screenings.md, section 3.3).

The changes register (film-changes.md) doesn't replace either section. Its only change to film-screenings.md is the maximum audience in section 3.1.
