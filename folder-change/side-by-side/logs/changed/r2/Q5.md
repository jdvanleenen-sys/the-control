# Q5, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "child|under 1|ticket" in reference/film
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The child's ticket is £3, not £2. Children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult (film-fees.md, section 2.3). The child is 9 and the parent accompanies them throughout, so this applies.

The £2 on the old price list isn't in the governing fee rules. I didn't open the archive, so I can't say where that figure comes from.

No change in film-changes.md replaces section 2.3. The only ticket-price amendment, 3.1 of 19 January 2026, replaces section 2.2 (member admission), so it doesn't affect the child's price.
