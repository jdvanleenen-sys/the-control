# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|supervis|child|under 1[0-9]|capacity" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that count includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, 4.2 and 4.4). Your event has 21 (16 children and 5 adults).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, 3.3). All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers them. The rules do not say how close the adults must be or what counts as supervision, so I can't say whether adults in a nearby room would satisfy it. They do not allow the children to be without adult supervision.

No entry in hall-changes.md replaces either section for 19 October 2025.
