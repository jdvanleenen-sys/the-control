## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no. They don't specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). They also don't say whether a remote trustee counts towards the quorum.

Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). The three in-person trustees are one short of that. Whether the meeting is quorate depends on whether the video-call trustee counts as one of the four.

- **If remote attendance counts as present:** four trustees would meet the quorum.
- **If it doesn't count:** only three are present and the quorum isn't met.

The documents give no basis for choosing between these. The hall-changes.md document has no entry on quorum or remote attendance, so nothing replaces sections 4.2 or 6.3.
