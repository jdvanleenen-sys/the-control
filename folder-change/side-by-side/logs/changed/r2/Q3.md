# Q3, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep "conduct|warning|leave|suspen" in reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A serious threat may result in immediate removal without a warning (film-conduct.md, section 2.3).

Otherwise, a person who refuses to stop disruptive behaviour after being asked may be required to leave (film-conduct.md, sections 2.1–2.2). That route involves asking the person to stop first. Section 2.3 lets the volunteer skip that step for a serious threat.

film-changes.md has no amendment to the conduct rules, so section 2.3 applies on 14 November 2025.
