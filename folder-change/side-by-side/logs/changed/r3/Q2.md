# Q2, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

- **First person:** The payment was received on 30 June 2025, before 1 July 2025, so the old £24 fee applies (film-changes.md, section 1.4).
- **Second person:** The payment was received on 2 July 2025, after the change took effect, so the fee is £28 (film-changes.md, sections 1.2 and 1.3).

Decision 1 of film-changes.md replaces the £24 fee in film-fees.md, section 1.1. From 1 July 2025 the fee is £28, and the date the payment is received decides which fee applies.
