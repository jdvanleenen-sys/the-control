# Q8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify.** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). This group has nine, so the registration doesn't make up the difference. The 20% discount in section 3.1 isn't replaced for this date. The changes register's 25% rate (hall-changes.md, section 3) only applies to events from 1 February 2026, and the event is on 15 October 2025.

**Room-hire charge without the discount: £48.** The Main Hall is £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, section 2.1) for two paid hours, which is also the minimum booking (section 2.4). The £28 rate in hall-changes.md, section 2 only applies to events from 1 November 2025, so it doesn't apply to 15 October 2025.
