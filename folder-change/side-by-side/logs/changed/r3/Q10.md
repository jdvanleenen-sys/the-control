## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either question.

- **Remote trustee and quorum:** The rules don't specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). Nothing in them says a trustee on a video call counts towards quorum. The changes document (hall-changes.md) has nothing on remote attendance or quorum either.
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). It turns on whether the remote trustee counts.
  - If the remote trustee counts, there are four, which meets quorum.
  - If the remote trustee doesn't count, there are three, which is short of quorum.

  The documents don't say which applies.
