# Q6, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is £25. That is £10 for the portable speaker plus £15 for the three sets of display boards.

- The portable speaker costs £10 per booking (hall-fees.md, section 4.2).
- Folding display boards cost £5 per set per booking, so three sets cost £15 (hall-fees.md, section 4.3).

No changes entry replaces the equipment charges. The register only replaces hall-fees.md sections 2.1 and 3.1 among the fee sections, so sections 4.2 and 4.3 apply as written. The charges also apply to this booking because 6 September 2025 is after the 1 April 2025 start date (hall-fees.md, section 1.2).
