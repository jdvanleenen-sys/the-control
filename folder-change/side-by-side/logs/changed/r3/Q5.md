# Q5, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep "child|under 1|ticket" in reference/film
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

**The child's ticket is £3.** Children aged under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult (film-fees.md, section 2.3). The child is 9 and the parent accompanies them throughout, so this applies.

The volunteer's £2 price list does not govern. No changes document replaces section 2.3. The only ticket-price change, film-changes.md section 3, covers member admission (section 2.2) and does not affect child tickets.
