# Q3, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep "conduct|warning|remov|leave" in reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A serious threat may lead to immediate removal without a warning (film-conduct.md, section 2.3). The usual step is to ask the person to stop, and removal follows only if they refuse (film-conduct.md, sections 2.1 and 2.2). That sequence does not apply to serious threats.

I found no entry in film-changes.md that replaces this section.
