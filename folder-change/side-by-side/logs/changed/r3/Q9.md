# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep "Meeting Room|child|supervis|capacity|occupancy" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**Attendance: not permitted as planned.** 16 children plus 5 adults is 21 people, which is within the Meeting Room maximum of 24. Capacities include organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). So the attendance is permitted, and I should correct my first line: the numbers are within the limit.

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). Children aged 8 to 11 are covered, so they can't be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room.

Nothing in hall-changes.md replaces either section, so the original wording applies on 19 October 2025.
