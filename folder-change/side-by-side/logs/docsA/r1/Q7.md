# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, not 21:45.**

- The general rule in `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1 set a 22:00 cutoff for Friday and Saturday amplified music. The hirer is quoting that rule.
- A trustee decision made on 20 March 2026 replaced section 4.1 (`hall-changes.md` section 5). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played (section 5.3). That date is 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies.
- The quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md` section 5) also says 21:30.

The contract's 21:45 end time doesn't change this. I found no provision in the hall files that lets a booking contract override the cutoff. The band would be 15 minutes over, and the booking officer can require noise reduction under section 4.5. The contract should be amended to a 21:30 finish, and the hirer should be told that the 22:00 figure is out of date.
