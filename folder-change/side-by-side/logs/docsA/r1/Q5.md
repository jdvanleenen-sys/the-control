# Q5, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (6)

1. Grep Fenwray
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-screenings.md
6. Read reference/film/film-fees-archive.md

## Answer

The correct ticket price is **£3**.

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That section was effective from 1 March 2025. A 9-year-old with a parent for the whole screening qualifies.
- **The £2 price list is out of date:** The volunteer's £2 figure matches section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was fully superseded on 1 March 2025 and is marked "must not be used for current charges".
- **No later change affects it:** The committee changes register has no amendment to the child ticket price. Its only ticket-price change is for members, from 1 February 2026, and that doesn't apply to a child's ticket. A screening on 16 August 2025 falls under the current `film-fees.md`.
