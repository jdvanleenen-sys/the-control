# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep "Fenwray" in .
2. Glob "**/*" in .
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md
5. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

The first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Fee change:** The committee decision of 16 June 2025 raised annual adult membership from £24 to £28 from 1 July 2025 (`film-changes.md` §1.2). The date the payment is received sets the fee, and payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (§1.3–1.4).
- **First person:** The treasurer received their payment on 30 June 2025, which is before the change. Their fee is the original £24 (`film-fees.md` §1.1), so the £24 they paid is correct.
- **Second person:** The treasurer received their payment on 2 July 2025, after the change. Their fee is £28.

The membership rules say payment is due only after an application is accepted (`film-membership.md` §2.5). Membership starts on the date the treasurer receives payment (§3.1). Neither of these changes the amounts.
