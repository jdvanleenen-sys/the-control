# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2).
- Capacity counts organisers and children (4.4). The event has 16 children and 5 adults, so 21 people, which is under the limit.
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall is open 09:00–21:00 (3.3). All guests must be out by 21:00, and setup and clearing must fall within booked hours (3.4, 3.5).
- The committee changes register doesn't alter any of these provisions for this date.

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All the children are 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. The rules make no exception for short absences or for adults working in another room.
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (3.4).
- The hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).

**In practice:** the adults can split up, but at least one must stay with the children at all times. One adult must also remain near the main entrance. That could be the same adult if the Meeting Room is by the entrance, but the rules don't say so. With five adults, two or three can prepare the other room while the rest supervise.

The rules don't define "supervision". Whether an adult in an adjoining room could count is unclear, so the safe reading is that an adult stays with the children.

Separately, the hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2). They must also explain the emergency exits to the organisers (1.3).
