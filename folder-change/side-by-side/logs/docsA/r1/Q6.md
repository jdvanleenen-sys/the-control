# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Grep "speaker|display board|equipment" in reference/hall

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

- **Rates:** The 6 September 2025 booking is after 1 April 2025, so the current `hall-fees.md` charges apply, not the older archive rates. Section 4 sets the portable speaker at £10 per booking and folding display boards at £5 per set per booking.
- **Calculation:** One speaker is £10. Three sets of boards at £5 each is £15. That makes £25.
- **Changes register:** None of its entries change the equipment charges.
- **Discount:** The registered-group discount doesn't apply to equipment (section 3.4), so it doesn't affect this total.

The equipment can only be hired alongside the Meeting Room, which this booking includes. It also has to be requested at booking and confirmed as available.
