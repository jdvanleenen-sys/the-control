## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`), for a 19:30 start on 18 November 2025:

- **Seat held until 19:40.** Section 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors normally open at 19:00.** Section 3.3 says doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start.

I also checked the committee changes register. None of its amendments touch sections 3.3 or 3.4. The only change to section 3 is the maximum audience, which doesn't affect these times.
