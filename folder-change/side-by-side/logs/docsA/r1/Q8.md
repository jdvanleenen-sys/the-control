# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**It doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Under `hall-fees.md` §3.2, a registered village group must have at least ten local members. Being registered locally isn't enough, and nine members is one short. The 20% discount in §3.1 therefore doesn't apply.
- **Rate:** The booking is for 15 October 2025. The Main Hall rate rise to £28 per hour (`hall-changes.md` §2) only takes effect for events on or after 1 November 2025. The original rate of £24 per hour (`hall-fees.md` §2.1) applies.
- **Charge:** Two paid hours at £24 per hour is **£48**. Two hours also meets the two-hour minimum in §2.4.

The £48 covers room hire only. A kitchen, any equipment and the £30 booking deposit would be separate, and the deposit counts towards the total hire charge (§5.4).
