# Q1, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Fenwray
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/film/film-screenings.md
4. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md
5. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules, for a screening advertised at 19:30 on 18 November 2025:

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40**. Section 3.4 of `film-screenings.md` says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**. Section 3.3 says they open 30 minutes before the advertised start.

The committee changes register has no amendment to either rule. Its only change to `film-screenings.md` is the audience limit, which doesn't affect this question.

Section 3.5 adds that someone who arrives late may enter only during a suitable break.
