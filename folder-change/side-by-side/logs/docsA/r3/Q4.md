## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. They pay **£7**, the standard admission price.

- **No member price during suspension.** Membership Rules 5.4 (`film-membership.md`) says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The member's annual membership is still active, but 8 November 2025 falls inside the suspension from 1 to 20 November. The suspension is also within the 30-day maximum in Conduct Rules 4.1.
- **Standard admission applies instead.** Fees 2.1 (`film-fees.md`) sets standard admission at £7 per screening. The member is 35, so none of the child rates in 2.3 or 2.4 apply.
- **The 2026 price change doesn't affect this.** The committee's 19 January 2026 decision raised the member price from £4 to £5, but only for screenings from 1 February 2026. The screening date decides the price, so the change doesn't apply to an 8 November 2025 screening. The member price would have been £4 then, but it isn't available to them.

I'm assuming the suspension was validly made, with the committee vote and written decision that Conduct Rules 4.2 and 4.3 require. Nothing you've told me suggests otherwise.
