> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Fee rule:** Adult membership cost £24 under `film-fees.md` §1.1. The committee's decision of 16 June 2025 (`film-changes.md` §1) raised it to £28 from 1 July 2025.
- **Which fee applies:** The date the treasurer receives the payment decides the fee (`film-changes.md` §1.3). Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (§1.4).
- **First person:** Their payment was received on 30 June 2025, before the change, so £24 is correct.
- **Second person:** Their payment was received on 2 July 2025, after the change, so the fee is £28.

The membership rules say payment is due only after an application is accepted (`film-membership.md` §2.5). Both applications were already accepted, so the date of acceptance doesn't change the fee.
