# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2). That limit includes organisers and children (4.4). Your event has 16 children and 5 adults, so 21 people, which is 3 under the limit. The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2). The fire safety rules also apply (hall-bookings.md 4.5).

The date, 19 October 2025, is a Sunday. Sunday hours are 09:00 to 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3), so setup, the event and clearing must all fall within those hours. The committee changes register has no entry that affects this event.

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Hall-safety.md 3.3 says children under 12 must remain under adult supervision. All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. No exception is stated for short absences or for adults preparing another room.

In practice, not all the adults can go off to prepare the other room:
- At least one adult must stay with the children at all times.
- Hall-safety.md 3.4 also requires at least one responsible adult to remain near the main entrance. The rules don't say whether one adult can cover both duties. Safest is to keep an adult with the children and, if they aren't near the entrance, have another adult stationed there.
- The named hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3). They are also responsible for guests (1.2).

Preparation in the other room should happen within booked hours (hall-bookings.md 3.5). Any other room used must be booked and approved in writing. That isn't addressed in the question, but under hall-bookings.md 6.1 and 2.4 it would need to be.

I didn't rely on the quick guide because it is only a summary, though it agrees on the 24-person limit.
