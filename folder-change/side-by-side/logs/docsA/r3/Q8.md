## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Section 3.2 of `hall-fees.md` says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. Nine falls short. Being registered locally isn't enough on its own.
- **Charge:** The event is on 15 October 2025. The committee raised the Main Hall rate to £28 an hour, but only for events from 1 November 2025 (`hall-changes.md` section 2). So the original £24 an hour applies (`hall-fees.md` 2.1). Two hours is the minimum paid booking (2.4), so the charge is 2 × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. A kitchen, equipment or deposits would be extra.

The 25% discount change (effective 1 February 2026) doesn't apply to this event either. It wouldn't change the answer, because the ten-member minimum is unchanged.
