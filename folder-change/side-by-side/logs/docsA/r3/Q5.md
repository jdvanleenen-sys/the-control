## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-fees-archive.md

## Answer

The correct ticket price is **£3**.

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` (effective 1 March 2025) says children under 12 pay £3 when an adult accompanies them. The 9-year-old is under 12 and the parent is with them throughout, so this applies.
- **The old price list:** The volunteer's £2 figure matches section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025, and the archive says it "must not be used for current charges". It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025, before the 16 August 2025 screening.
- **Later amendments:** `film-changes.md` has no amendment to the child ticket price. Its ticket-price change (member admission, from £4 to £5) only takes effect on 1 February 2026 and doesn't affect children.
