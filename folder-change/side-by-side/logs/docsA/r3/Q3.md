# Q3, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (5)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Fenwray
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md

## Answer

**No. A warning isn't required for a serious threat.**

Under `film-conduct.md` section 2.3, "Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning." Threats are prohibited under 1.2. The visitor's lack of a previous warning doesn't change that.

- **Discretion:** The rule says "may", so removal is allowed but not mandatory. The duty volunteer can remove the visitor straight away or choose to ask them to stop first (2.1).
- **Record:** The duty volunteer must record the incident within 48 hours (2.4).
- **Membership:** Immediate removal doesn't end any society membership (2.5).
- **Refund:** Being removed doesn't automatically give the visitor a refund right (5.3). Eligibility is decided under `film-refunds.md` (5.4).

The 14 November 2025 date doesn't matter here. `film-changes.md` doesn't amend the conduct rules, which took effect on 1 April 2025.
