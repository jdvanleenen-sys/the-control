# Q6, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

5. Read reference/hall/hall-quick-guide.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

Under hall-fees.md section 4, the charges are:
- One portable speaker costs £10 per booking (4.2).
- Three sets of folding display boards cost 3 × £5 = £15 (4.3).

A 6 September 2025 booking falls under the current fee rules, because they apply to bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025 (1.2). The committee changes register has no change to equipment charges. Its only fee changes are the Main Hall rate and the village group discount, and both start later.

The village group discount never applies to equipment anyway (3.4).
