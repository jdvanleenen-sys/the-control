# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is 15 minutes too late.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, does say Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 22:00. The hirer is quoting that version.
- The committee changes register (`hall-changes.md`, section 5) replaced 4.1 with effect from 1 April 2026. The new rule is that Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played, not the date of booking or the contract. The event is on Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after 1 April, so the 21:30 rule applies.
- The quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md`, section 5) also says 21:30.

The contract's 21:45 end time doesn't override the hall rules. The band should finish by 21:30, or the hirer should ask the hall trustees whether an exception is possible.
