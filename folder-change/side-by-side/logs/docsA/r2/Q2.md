> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- **Fee basis:** Annual adult membership was £24 (film-fees.md 1.1). The committee decision of 16 June 2025 raised it to £28 from 1 July 2025 (film-changes.md 1.2). The date the treasurer receives the payment sets the fee (film-changes.md 1.3), and payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (film-changes.md 1.4).
- **First person:** Their payment was received on 30 June 2025, before the change, so the fee is £24. That matches what they paid.
- **Second person:** Their payment was received on 2 July 2025, after the change, so the fee is £28.

The date of acceptance doesn't matter here. Payment is only due after acceptance (film-membership.md 2.5), and the fee depends on when the payment is received.
