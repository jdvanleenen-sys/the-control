## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is not allowed.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` 4.1, set the Friday and Saturday cutoff at 22:00. That's the rule the hirer is relying on.
- A committee decision of 20 March 2026 replaced it (`hall-changes.md` 5.1–5.3). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30. The quick guide says the same.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played. That is 4 April 2026, a Saturday, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the new 21:30 cutoff applies.

The band's contract and the 22:00 wording in the general rules are both out of date. The band should finish by 21:30, which is 15 minutes earlier than the contract says. The hall can't override the cutoff by agreeing to the contract time. The booking officer can also require immediate noise reduction (4.5), so overrunning could be enforced on the night.
