# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2). That limit includes organisers and children (4.4). With 16 children and 5 adults, there will be 21 people, which is 3 under the limit. The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2). The fire safety rules also apply (hall-bookings.md 4.5).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All 16 children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. The rules make no exception for short periods or for the adults being busy elsewhere.
- At least one responsible adult must also remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). This is a separate requirement. The adult at the entrance can't also be counted on to supervise the children unless they can do both from where they are. The rules don't say so either way.

**In practice:** not all five adults can go off to prepare the other room. At least one adult must stay with the children. Another must stay near the main entrance, unless one adult can properly do both. The other adults are free to prepare the room. The rules set no adult-to-child ratio, so "supervision" is not defined further.

**Other points that may matter**
- The named hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.2–1.3).
- Fire exits and corridors must stay clear while the other room is being prepared (hall-safety.md 2.1, 3.5).
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall closes at 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3). Setup and clearing must fall within the booked hours (3.5).
- The Committee Changes Register has no entries affecting capacity or supervision. Its changes also take effect from 1 November 2025 or later, so none apply to this date.
