# Q5, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Steps (6)

1. Glob **/*
2. Grep Fenwray
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-fees-archive.md
6. Grep "child|under 12|accompan" in reference/film

## Answer

The correct ticket price for the child is **£3**.

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That fee schedule was adopted on 12 February 2025 and took effect on 1 March 2025, so it applies on 16 August 2025. A 9-year-old with a parent present meets both conditions.
- **The volunteer's price list:** The £2 figure comes from the archived schedule, `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025 and was superseded in full on 1 March 2025. The archive says it "must not be used for current charges."
- **Later changes:** The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) amends adult membership, member admission, the equipment deposit, maximum hire, audience limit and refund notice. None of these changes the under-12 ticket price.
