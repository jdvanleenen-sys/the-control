## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**No, the group doesn't qualify for the discount. The room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** Hall fees section 3.2 says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. Being registered locally isn't enough, and nine members is one short. The 25% rate from the changes register wouldn't apply anyway. It only takes effect for events from 1 February 2026, and this event is on 15 October 2025.
- **Rate:** The Main Hall rate is £24 per hour (section 2.1). The rise to £28 only applies to events starting on or after 1 November 2025 (changes register, section 2), so it doesn't affect 15 October 2025.
- **Charge:** Two paid hours at £24 is £48. Two hours is also the minimum paid booking (section 2.4).

The £48 covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit counts towards it (section 5.4). Any kitchen or equipment charges would be extra.
