> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`):

- **Seat hold:** Reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start (rule 3.4). For a 19:30 start on 18 November 2025, the society must hold the seat until **19:40**.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open 30 minutes before the advertised start (rule 3.3). That means **19:00**.

The committee changes register doesn't amend either rule. It only changes the maximum audience, fees, deposits, hire length and refund notice.

If the person arrives after 19:40, the society no longer has to hold the seat. Rule 3.5 also says late arrivals can enter only during a suitable break.
