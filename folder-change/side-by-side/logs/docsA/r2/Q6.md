# Q6, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Quillbrook|portable speaker|display board
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
5. Grep "speaker|board|equipment|Meeting Room" in reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

The rates come from `hall-fees.md` section 4. They apply because the booking starts after 1 April 2025.
- One portable speaker costs £10 per booking (4.2).
- Three sets of folding display boards cost 3 × £5 = £15 per booking (4.3).

The committee changes register doesn't alter any equipment charge. Its changes only cover the Main Hall rate, the village group discount, opening hours, music cutoff, cancellation refunds and the emergency spending limit. The group discount doesn't apply to equipment either (3.4).

The fees document says equipment is only hired alongside a room, which the Meeting Room booking satisfies. It also says equipment availability must be confirmed and requested when the booking is made (`hall-bookings.md` 5.3). I assumed the equipment is available.
