## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member price. They pay the standard admission of £7.**

- **Suspension:** Under `film-membership.md` §5.4, "a suspended member cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so it covers the screening on 8 November. The active annual membership doesn't change this, because the member price is blocked while the suspension lasts.
- **Member price:** `film-fees.md` §2.2 gives the member price (£4) only to "members with active membership". The member in this case is suspended on the screening date, so they can't claim it. The committee's later change to £5 only applies from 1 February 2026, so it doesn't affect a November 2025 screening.
- **Price that applies:** `film-fees.md` §2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening. The £3 and £5 child rates don't apply to someone aged 35.

The suspension only blocks member prices, not entry. Admission still depends on capacity (`film-screenings.md` §3), which was 42 people at that date. Once the suspension ends on 20 November, the member price would be available again.

I haven't read `film-conduct.md` §4, which governs suspensions. It might add other restrictions, but nothing in the membership or fee rules changes the answer above.
