# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

- **First person (payment received 30 June 2025):** £24. The fee rises to £28 from 1 July 2025. The date the payment is received sets the fee, and payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (film-changes.md, sections 1.2–1.4, replacing film-fees.md, section 1.1).
- **Second person (payment received 2 July 2025):** £28. This is £4 more than the £24 in film-fees.md, section 1.1, because it was received after the change took effect (film-changes.md, sections 1.2–1.3).

Under film-fees.md, section 4.5, a payment counts as received when cash is handed over or a bank transfer clears. I took "receives" in your question to mean that.
