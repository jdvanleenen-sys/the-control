# Q1, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Fenwray
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/film/film-screenings.md
4. Read reference/film/film-quick-guide.md
5. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the society must hold the seat until **19:40** on 18 November 2025. Doors normally open at **19:00**.

- **Seat hold:** Section 3.4 of `film-screenings.md` says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start. That is 19:30 + 10 minutes = 19:40.
- **Doors:** Section 3.3 says doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start. That is 19:30 − 30 minutes = 19:00.

The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) amends the maximum audience in 3.1 but not 3.3 or 3.4, so both rules stand as written.

If the person arrives after 19:40, the seat is no longer held. Under 3.5 they could still enter, but only during a suitable break in the film.
