## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

The first person pays **£24** and the second person pays **£28**.

- **Rule that sets the fee:** Under film-fees.md §1.1, annual adult membership costs £24. The committee changes register (film-changes.md §1) replaced that with £28 for payments received from 1 July 2025. The fee depends on the date the treasurer receives the payment (§1.3). Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (§1.4).
- **First person:** Their payment was received on 30 June 2025, which is before 1 July. The £24 fee applies, so their £24 payment is correct.
- **Second person:** Their payment was received on 2 July 2025, which is after the change. The fee is £28, so they pay £28.

The date the applications were accepted makes no difference. Payment only falls due after acceptance (film-membership.md §2.5), and the fee is set by the date the payment is received.
