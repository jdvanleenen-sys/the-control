# H2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: An adult member booked the projector on 20 September 2025, paid the required hire fee and security deposit, and received confirmation that day. Collection is scheduled for 3 October 2025. How much security deposit must the member ultimately provide before collecting it?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-equipment.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

The member must provide a total deposit of £100. That is £20 more than the £80 they paid.

The decision of 10 September 2025 replaces the £80 deposit with £100 from 1 October 2025, and the scheduled collection date decides which deposit applies (film-changes.md, section 2.2–2.3). Collection is on 3 October 2025, so the £100 deposit applies. Earlier confirmed bookings are subject to the new deposit if collected on or after 1 October 2025 (film-changes.md, section 2.4). Confirmation on 20 September 2025 does not keep the old £80 figure (film-equipment.md, section 3.1, replaced).
