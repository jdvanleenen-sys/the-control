## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: On 12 May 2025, a volunteer takes a £9 cash payment for a screening ticket. The customer asks whether the treasurer is required to issue a receipt. Is a receipt compulsory, and what is the payment threshold?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "receipt" in reference/film
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

No, a receipt is not compulsory for this payment. The threshold is £10. The treasurer issues a receipt for payments of £10 or more (film-fees.md, section 4.4), and £9 is £1 below that.

The documents don't say whether a receipt may be given for a payment under £10, so I can't say either way.
