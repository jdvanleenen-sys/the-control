## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: An adult member booked the projector on 20 September 2025, paid the required hire fee and security deposit, and received confirmation that day. Collection is scheduled for 3 October 2025. How much security deposit must the member ultimately provide before collecting it?

## Answer

The member must provide a **£100** security deposit in total before collecting the projector.

- The original rule in `film-equipment.md` §3.1 set the deposit at £80 per booking.
- The committee's decision of 10 September 2025 (`film-changes.md` §2) raised it to £100 from 1 October 2025.
- Under §2.3, the scheduled collection date decides which deposit applies, not the booking or payment date.
- Under §2.4, a booking confirmed earlier is still subject to the new deposit if collection is on or after 1 October 2025.
- Collection is on 3 October 2025, so the £100 deposit applies.

The member paid £80 when booking, so they owe a further **£20** before collection. The rules don't say how or when that top-up is collected, so I'm inferring that it's paid at or before collection.
