Does the folder earn its place?

A folder of instructions is meant to help an AI answer questions about a set of documents. This test checked whether it does.

No. The folder got the AI to its answers in half the steps, but its answers were marked right less often than with the documents alone.

The experiment

The same 10 questions, asked of the same AI, in three set-ups. Each set-up was asked every question 3 times.

Why the folder lost marks

Our reading, which is not proved. The folder's rules and examples push for short answers. Its answers were right on the main point and less than half the length. The answer key also wanted the detail a longer answer includes, such as how far over a limit something was.

The follow-up: one line, tested on a copy

After the verdict, one line was added to a copy of the folder's rules: "Say what would otherwise apply, and the difference." Then the three set-ups were run again, on the 10 exam questions and on the 10 kept aside.

The lost marks came back30 of 30 on all three gradings. It was 23 to 28 on the exam. The 10 kept aside were already 30 of 30, and stayed there.
The steps did not move2.33 against 5.20. Fewer on 10 of 10.
The text is still more9,579 against 7,665. By the same rule, still not earned.

The line was written after seeing which answers lost marks, so it is fitted to those questions. The first verdict stands.

What held up
  • Most of the saving on steps comes from the map: 2.50 with the map alone, 6.07 with nothing loaded.
  • A re-run on a second computer, set up from nothing, gave the same result on steps: 2.30 against 5.20.
  • On 10 questions kept aside: fewer steps on all 10, and 30 of 30 marked right in both set-ups. The loss of marks did not repeat.
What this does not show
  • One AI model, made-up documents, ten questions, one day.
  • The graders could tell the two set-ups apart from the writing.
  • The second computer used the same account. Nobody outside has run it yet.

The design

How the test was built. What makes it an experiment and not a demonstration.

The one thing changedWhether the folder's 4 instruction files are loaded before the question.
Kept the sameThe AI model and its settings, the 18 documents, the 10 questions, the tools (search, list, open), and a fresh session for every question.
What was measuredSteps taken, answers marked right, text put in front of the AI, tokens and seconds.
The controlsNothing at all. The documents alone. The folder present but not loaded. A folder with its map made wrong on purpose.
The sample10 questions, asked 3 times each, in 19 set-ups: 570 sessions. The 19 are every mix of the 4 instruction files loaded or not (16), the documents alone, nothing, and a folder with a wrong map. Then 10 more questions held back as a check.
The decision ruleA difference counts only if it shows on 9 or 10 of the 10 questions. Written down before any document existed.
