# Q9, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

2. Glob **/*{quillbrook,Quillbrook,hall,Hall,rules,Rules}*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2).
- Capacity counts organisers and children (4.4).
- 16 children plus 5 adults is 21, which is 3 under the limit.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). All the children are 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. There is no exception for short periods or for adults working in another room.
- Adults can still prepare the other room, but at least one adult must stay with the children. With five adults, that is workable.
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (3.4). The same adult can do both jobs if they can supervise the children from that position. If not, you need two adults on duty.
- The hirer must stay on site for the whole event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).
- Keep corridors and access routes clear while the room is being prepared (hall-safety.md 3.5).
- A serious breach can lead to the event being ended immediately, with no refund of room hire (hall-conduct.md 6.1 and 6.2).

**Other points**
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall closes at 21:00 and amplified music must stop by 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3, hall-conduct.md 4.2).
- None of the entries in hall-changes.md alter these rules for that date.
- Today is 12 October 2026, so that date has already passed. If you meant a 2026 date, the answer is the same, because none of the changes touch capacity or supervision. The Sunday hours would also be the same if the new date is a Sunday. Check the weekday if it isn't.
