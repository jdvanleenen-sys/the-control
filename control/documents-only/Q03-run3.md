## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Answer

**No. The duty volunteer doesn't have to warn the visitor first.**

Section 2.3 of the Conduct and Complaints rules (`film-conduct.md`) says "Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning." Section 1.2 prohibits threats. The 14 November 2025 screening falls after the rules took effect on 1 April 2025. The changes register doesn't amend the conduct rules, so section 2.3 applies as written.

- **Discretion:** Section 2.3 says removal "may" happen immediately, so the volunteer can remove the visitor at once without issuing a warning.
- **Ordinary disruption:** For lesser disruption, sections 2.1 and 2.2 apply. The volunteer asks the person to stop, and they can be required to leave only if they refuse. That two-step process doesn't apply to a serious threat.
- **Record:** The volunteer must record the incident within 48 hours (section 2.4).
- **Refund:** Removal doesn't automatically create a refund right (section 5.3). Refund eligibility is decided under `film-refunds.md`.
