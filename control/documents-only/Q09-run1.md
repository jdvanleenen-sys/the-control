# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** There are 16 children and 5 adults, so 21 people in total. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 (hall-bookings.md 4.2), and that figure includes organisers and children (4.4). The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). Children aged 8 to 11 are all under 12, so the rule covers every child in the group. The rules allow no exception for short periods, such as while the adults prepare another room.
- At least one responsible adult must also remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). Whether that adult can be the one supervising the children depends on where the Meeting Room is relative to the entrance, and the rules don't say.
- The hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).

With five adults, some can prepare the other room as long as at least one stays with the children. If that adult is not near the main entrance, a second adult must cover the entrance.

**Other points to check:**
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the event and any setup and clearing must finish by 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3–3.5).
- The other room must also be booked, and it must be within booked hours. Setup must take place within booked hours (3.5).
- The hirer must explain the emergency exits to the organisers (hall-safety.md 1.3).
- None of the committee changes in hall-changes.md alter these sections.
- The date is in the past relative to today (12 October 2026). If you meant 2025 as the event year, this answer applies as of that date. If the event is actually upcoming, check the date again.
