> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Answer

**No. A warning isn't required.** Section 2.3 of the Fenwray Film Society conduct rules (`reference/film/film-conduct.md`) says "Serious threats may result in immediate removal without a warning." So the duty volunteer can require the visitor to leave straight away, even though they had no earlier warning.

- **Date:** The conduct rules were effective from 1 April 2025, so they applied on 14 November 2025. The committee changes register has no amendment to the conduct rules.
- **Wording:** Section 2.3 says removal "may" follow. It permits immediate removal but doesn't require it. The usual route in 2.1 and 2.2 is to ask the person to stop and then require them to leave if they refuse. Serious threats skip that step.
- **Follow-up:** Under 2.4, the duty volunteer must record the incident within 48 hours. Under 2.5, removal doesn't by itself end the visitor's membership. Under 5.3, it doesn't automatically create a refund right either.
