> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society's screening rules (`film-screenings.md`), for a screening advertised at 19:30 on 18 November 2025:

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40**. Section 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**. Section 3.3 says doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start.

The changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to sections 3.3 or 3.4. Its one change to `film-screenings.md` is the audience cap, which doesn't affect these times. After 19:40 the seat is no longer held. Under section 3.5, a late arrival can enter only during a suitable break.
