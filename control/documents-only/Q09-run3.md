# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that figure includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md 4.2 and 4.4). Here there are 16 children plus 5 adults, which makes 21, so there are 3 spaces to spare. No committee change in hall-changes.md alters capacity or the rules below. 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the event must also end by 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3 and 3.4).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Hall-safety.md 3.3 says children under 12 must remain under adult supervision. All of the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them. It has no exception for short absences or for preparing another room.
- Hall-safety.md 3.4 also requires at least one responsible adult to remain near the main entrance. The rules don't say whether that adult can be the same person who supervises the children, so I wouldn't assume so unless the Meeting Room is next to the entrance.
- Hall-conduct.md 1.3 requires the hirer to stay on site throughout the event.

**What the organiser can do:** Keep at least one adult with the children at all times, and make sure an adult is also near the entrance. The other adults can prepare the other room. With five adults, that is workable.

**Related points:**
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).
- The hirer must explain the emergency exits to the organisers (hall-safety.md 1.3).
- Using another room means it must be part of the booking. If it is booked as a combined event, the limit is 100 people in the building (hall-bookings.md 4.3).
- Setup must fall within booked hours (hall-bookings.md 3.5).
