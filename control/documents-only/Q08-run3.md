## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**No, it doesn't qualify.** Under hall-fees.md §3.2, a registered village group needs at least ten local members, and this group has nine. Being registered locally isn't enough on its own.

**Room-hire charge without the discount: £48.**
- The Main Hall is £24 per hour (§2.1). The committee's increase to £28 only takes effect for events starting on or after 1 November 2025 (hall-changes.md §2), so it doesn't apply to a 15 October 2025 event.
- Two paid hours is the minimum booking (§2.4), so the charge is 2 × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. The £30 booking deposit (§5.1) counts towards the total, and the kitchen and any equipment are charged separately.
