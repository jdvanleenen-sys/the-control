# H6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: At a trustee committee meeting on 17 September 2025, seven trustees are in office but only three attend. One attendee says that three should be enough because all three agree. What is the required quorum, and can the committee make a valid decision at this meeting?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The required quorum is four trustees, so this meeting can't make a valid decision. Only three trustees attend, which is below quorum, and that the three agree doesn't change this (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2: "Four trustees form a quorum").

The changes document (hall-changes.md) doesn't replace section 4.2.
