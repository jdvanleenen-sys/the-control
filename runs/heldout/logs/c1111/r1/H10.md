# H10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A trustee was elected in March 2022, re-elected in March 2024 and wants to stand again in March 2026. Is there a maximum number of consecutive two-year terms a trustee may serve, and what exactly is that maximum?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. The rules set no maximum number of consecutive terms, so there is no figure to give. A trustee serves two years "and may stand again", and the rules say they "do not set a maximum number of trustee terms" (hall-trustees.md, sections 2.4 and 6.1). hall-changes.md has no amendment on trustee terms.
