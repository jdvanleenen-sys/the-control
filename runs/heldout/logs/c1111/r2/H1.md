# H1, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: On 12 May 2025, a volunteer takes a £9 cash payment for a screening ticket. The customer asks whether the treasurer is required to issue a receipt. Is a receipt compulsory, and what is the payment threshold?

## Steps (1)

1. Grep reference/film

## Answer

No. A receipt isn't compulsory for a £9 payment, because the threshold is £10. The treasurer issues a receipt for payments of £10 or more (film-fees.md, section 4.4). The 12 May 2025 date doesn't change this, because film-changes.md doesn't replace section 4.4.
