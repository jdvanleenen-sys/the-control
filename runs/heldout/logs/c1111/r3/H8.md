# H8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: On 12 October 2025, an organiser wants to use both the Main Hall and Meeting Room for one event. The expected attendance is 89 people in the Main Hall and 23 in the Meeting Room, including organisers and children. Both rooms would individually be within their stated capacities. Is the combined event permitted at that attendance, and by how many people would it exceed any applicable limit?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md

## Answer

No. The combined event of 89 + 23 = 112 people would exceed the 100-person building limit by 12.

The rule is that combined events may not exceed 100 people in the building, and capacities include organisers, performers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.3 and 4.4). Each room is within its own limit: 90 for the Main Hall and 24 for the Meeting Room (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.1 and 4.2). The combined cap still applies.

hall-changes.md does not replace section 4, so this limit governs on 12 October 2025.
