> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. ## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either part. The rules don't specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, 6.3), and nothing in hall-changes.md covers remote attendance or quorum.

The quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, 4.2). The answer turns on whether a remote trustee counts as present, and the rules leave that open:
- **If the remote trustee counts:** four trustees would be present, which meets the quorum.
- **If the remote trustee doesn't count:** only three would be present, which falls short of the quorum.

The rules don't say which applies.
