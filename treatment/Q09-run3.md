# Q9, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md

## Answer

**Attendance:** Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 people, and capacity includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). Your event has 21 people (16 children and 5 adults).

**Leaving the children unsupervised:** No. Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision, and all the children here are aged 8 to 11 (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). The rules do not say how many adults are needed per child. They also do not say whether some adults may leave the room while others stay.

Separately, at least one responsible adult must remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md, section 3.4).

No entry in hall-changes.md replaces any of these sections.
