# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (3)

1. Grep reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance:** Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 people, and that figure includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). Your 16 children and 5 adults make 21.

**Leaving the children unsupervised:** No. Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule applies to every one of them. The rules don't allow an exception while adults prepare another room.

The changes document (hall-changes.md) does not replace any of these sections.
