> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either part.

- **Remote trustee counting towards quorum:** The rules state that they "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely" (hall-trustees.md, 6.3). Nothing in them says whether a trustee on a video call counts as present.
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** The quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, 4.2). The three in-person trustees are short of that on their own. Whether the meeting reaches four depends on whether the remote trustee counts, and the rules don't settle that.

I checked hall-changes.md and found no amendment on quorum or remote attendance.
