# Q3, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: At a screening on 14 November 2025, a visitor makes a serious threat against another attendee. The visitor has not previously been warned about their behaviour. Must the duty volunteer issue a warning before requiring the visitor to leave?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
2. Grep reference/film/film-changes.md

## Answer

No. A serious threat may lead to immediate removal without a warning, so the volunteer doesn't have to warn the visitor first (film-conduct.md, section 2.3). Section 2.3 says "may", so removal is permitted rather than required.

I checked film-changes.md and found no amendment to this section.
