# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-conduct.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Amplified music on 4 April 2026, a Saturday, must stop by 21:30, so the band can't play until 21:45.

Hall-changes.md section 5 replaces hall-conduct.md section 4.1 (the 22:00 cutoff) from 1 April 2026. The controlling date is the date the music is played, and 4 April is after 1 April. The hirer's 22:00 figure is the replaced wording.
