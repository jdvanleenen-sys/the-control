# Q8, run 2

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, it doesn't qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). The 25% change in hall-changes.md section 3 isn't effective until 1 February 2026, so it doesn't apply to an event on 15 October 2025. The change in section 3 doesn't alter the ten-member minimum either.

The room-hire charge without the discount is £48. That is two paid hours at £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, sections 2.1 and 2.4). The £28 rate in hall-changes.md section 2 only applies to events from 1 November 2025.
