# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Read reference/film/film-conduct.md
4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

No, they aren't entitled to the member price. They pay standard admission of £7.

- **No discount:** A suspended member cannot claim member prices (film-membership.md, 5.4). The suspension covers 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. The committee's power to suspend is in film-conduct.md, 4.1 and 4.4.
- **Price:** Standard admission is £7 per screening (film-fees.md, 2.1). The changes document replaces only the member rate in 2.2, and that change starts 1 February 2026 (film-changes.md, 3). It doesn't affect the standard price.

I'm treating the 35-year-old as an adult paying standard admission. The child rates in film-fees.md, 2.3 and 2.4, don't apply to them.
