# Q8, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

It does not qualify, and the room-hire charge without the discount is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). The 15 October 2025 event is before the 1 February 2026 effective date of the changes entry, so the original section 3.1 applies (hall-changes.md, section 3).
- **Charge:** The Main Hall rate on 15 October 2025 is £24 per hour, so two paid hours cost £48 (hall-fees.md, sections 2.1 and 2.4). The £28 rate takes effect only for events from 1 November 2025 (hall-changes.md, section 2).
