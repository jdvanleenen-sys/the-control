# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, the group doesn't qualify. Without the discount, the room-hire charge is £48.

- **Discount:** A registered village group must have at least ten local members (hall-fees.md, 3.2). This group has nine. The changes register doesn't alter this requirement: its only change to section 3.1 is the rate, and that takes effect on 1 February 2026, after the event.
- **Charge:** The event is on 15 October 2025, before the 1 November 2025 effective date of the £28 rate (hall-changes.md, 2.1–2.3). So the original Main Hall rate of £24 per hour applies (hall-fees.md, 2.1). Two paid hours at £24 is £48, which also meets the two-hour minimum (hall-fees.md, 2.4).
