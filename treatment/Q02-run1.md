# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
2. Read reference/film/film-fees.md

## Answer

The first person pays £24 and the second pays £28.

The fee depends on the date the payment is received. Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old £24 fee, and from 1 July 2025 the fee is £28 (film-changes.md, section 1, items 1.2–1.4, which replaces film-fees.md 1.1). The first payment was received on 30 June 2025, so £24 applies. The second was received on 2 July 2025, so £28 applies.
