# Q7, run 3

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-conduct.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Section 4.1 of hall-conduct.md (22:00 on Friday and Saturday) was replaced from 1 April 2026. Amplified music on a Friday or Saturday must now stop by 21:30, and the controlling date is the day the music is played (hall-changes.md, section 5). The band plays on Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the effective date, so 21:30 applies and 21:45 is too late.
