## Answer

The documents don't say, so I can't answer yes or no on either question.

- **Remote trustee counting towards quorum:** The rules don't specify whether trustees may attend remotely (hall-trustees.md, section 6.3). Nothing in them says whether a trustee on a video call is "present" or counts towards the quorum.
- **Whether the meeting is quorate:** Quorum is four trustees (hall-trustees.md, section 4.2). If the remote trustee counts, four are present and the meeting is quorate. If they don't count, only three are present and it isn't. The rules don't say which applies.
