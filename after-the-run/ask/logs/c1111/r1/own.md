# own, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: What does it cost to hire the Main Hall for three hours with the kitchen on 10 December 2025, for a hirer with no discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The Main Hall for three hours with the kitchen costs **£92**.

- The Main Hall rate is £28 per hour, not £24. The changes register replaces hall-fees.md 2.1 for events on or after 1 November 2025, and 10 December 2025 is after that date (hall-changes.md, section 2). Three hours at £28 is £84.
- The kitchen adds £8 per booking (hall-fees.md, 2.3).
- Three hours meets the two-hour minimum (hall-fees.md, 2.4) and needs no rounding (2.6).

Total: £84 + £8 = £92.
