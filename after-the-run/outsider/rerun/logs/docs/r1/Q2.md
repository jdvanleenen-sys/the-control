# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Steps (5)

1. Grep Fenwray
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
4. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
5. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

The first person pays **£24** and the second pays **£28**.

- The base rate in `film-fees.md` §1.1 is £24 for annual adult membership.
- The committee decision of 16 June 2025 (`film-changes.md` §1) raised it to £28 from 1 July 2025.
- The date the payment is received sets the fee (§1.3). Payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (§1.4).
- The first person's payment was received on 30 June 2025, so the old £24 fee applies.
- The second person's payment was received on 2 July 2025, so the new £28 fee applies.

The acceptance dates don't matter here, because payment is only due after acceptance (`film-membership.md` §2.5). The fee depends only on the date of payment.
