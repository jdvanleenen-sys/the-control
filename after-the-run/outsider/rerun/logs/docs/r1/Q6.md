# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

The current rules in `hall-fees.md` apply to a booking starting on 6 September 2025, because they cover bookings from 1 April 2025. None of the later committee changes touch equipment fees.

- Portable speaker: 1 × £10 per booking = £10 (section 4.2)
- Folding display boards: 3 sets × £5 per set per booking = £15 (section 4.3)

The group discount doesn't reduce equipment charges (section 3.4), and equipment is only hired alongside a room, which the Meeting Room booking covers (section 4.4).

6 September 2025 is a Saturday. `hall-conduct.md` section 4.3 says external speakers aren't permitted, but that is about speakers a hirer brings in. The hall's own portable speaker is a hireable item.

The hall must also confirm that the equipment is available (`hall-fees.md` section 4.5), and the hirer must request it when making the booking (`hall-bookings.md` section 5.3).
