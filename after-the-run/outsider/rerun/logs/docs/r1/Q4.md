## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. They pay the standard admission price of £7.**

- **Suspension covers the date.** 8 November 2025 falls inside the suspension period of 1–20 November 2025. The suspension is 20 days, within the 30-day maximum in `film-conduct.md` §4.1.
- **Suspended members can't claim member prices.** `film-membership.md` §5.4 says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." Having an active annual membership doesn't change this. `film-conduct.md` §4.4 sends questions about rights during suspension back to the membership rules.
- **£7 is the price that applies.** Standard admission is £7 per screening under `film-fees.md` §2.1. Their age of 35 gives no child or youth concession.
- **The 2026 change doesn't affect this.** `film-changes.md` raised the active-member price from £4 to £5 from 1 February 2026. That change doesn't apply to a November 2025 screening, and it wouldn't matter here anyway.

Membership isn't cancelled. It resumes after 20 November, and the suspension doesn't extend or refund any membership time.
