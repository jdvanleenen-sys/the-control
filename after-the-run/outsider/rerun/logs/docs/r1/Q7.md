## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No, the band may not play until 21:45.** Amplified music must stop by 21:30 on that Saturday.

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, set a 22:00 limit for Friday and Saturday. That is the rule the hirer is quoting.
- A committee decision of 20 March 2026 replaced section 4.1 (`hall-changes.md` section 5). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played. That date is 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April start, so the new limit applies.
- The band's contract was written for an earlier limit. It can't override the hall rules, so 21:45 is 15 minutes too late.
- The hirer is relying on the original wording. The hall's original documents keep that wording, but the changes register replaces it from the effective date. The quick guide (`hall-quick-guide.md` section 5) also says 21:30.

The band should plan to finish by 21:30. Under section 4.5, the booking officer can also require immediate noise reduction.
