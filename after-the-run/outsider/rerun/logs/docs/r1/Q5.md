> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

**The child's ticket is £3.**

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children aged under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That schedule took effect on 1 March 2025, so it covers the 16 August 2025 screening. A 9-year-old with a parent present qualifies.
- **The volunteer's £2 price:** This matches section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 to 28 February 2025 and was superseded in full on 1 March 2025. The archive says it must not be used for current charges.
- **Later changes:** The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to the child ticket price. Its only admission change is to the member price, from 1 February 2026.
