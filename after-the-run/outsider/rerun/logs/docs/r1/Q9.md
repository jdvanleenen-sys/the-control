# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.**
- The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2).
- That limit counts organisers and children (4.4).
- 16 children plus 5 adults is 21, so there are 3 places to spare.
- The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.**
- Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3).
- All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers every one of them.
- The adults can't all go off to prepare another room. At least one adult must stay with the children.
- Separately, at least one responsible adult must remain near the main entrance (3.4). The rules don't say whether one adult can cover both duties. The safer reading is to keep one adult with the children and have another at the entrance.
- The rules set no adult-to-child ratio, so they don't say how many adults must stay. With five adults, some can prepare the other room as long as the children stay supervised.
- The named hirer must also stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).

**Something you didn't ask about:** 19 October 2025 is a Sunday. Sunday hours are 09:00 to 21:00, and all guests must leave by closing (hall-bookings.md 3.3, 3.4). Setup and clearing must also fall within the booked hours (3.5). The committee changes register doesn't alter any of the sections above.
