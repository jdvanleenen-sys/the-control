# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**Discount: no.** Section 3.2 of `hall-fees.md` says a registered village group must have at least ten local members. This group has nine, so being registered locally isn't enough. The later change to the discount percentage (`hall-changes.md`, 25% from 1 February 2026) doesn't affect this, and it doesn't apply to a 2025 event anyway.

**Room-hire charge without the discount: £48.**
- The event is on 15 October 2025. The Main Hall increase to £28 an hour only applies to events on or after 1 November 2025, so the original rate of £24 an hour applies (section 2.1).
- Two paid hours (which is also the minimum under section 2.4) × £24 = **£48**.

This covers room hire only. A kitchen (£8), equipment, and the £30 booking deposit would be separate, and the deposit counts towards the total.
