# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, it doesn't qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). The 15 October 2025 event date is before the 1 November 2025 and 1 February 2026 effective dates, so the changes register doesn't alter either section 2.1 or section 3.1 for this booking.

Without the discount, the charge is £48. That is two paid hours at the Main Hall rate of £24 per hour (hall-fees.md, sections 2.1 and 2.4).
