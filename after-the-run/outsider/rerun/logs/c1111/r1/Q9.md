# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep "(?i)meeting room|child|supervis|capacity|occupancy" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md

## Answer

**Attendance:** Yes, it is permitted. The Meeting Room maximum is 24 people, and that figure includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, sections 4.2 and 4.4). Your event has 16 children plus 5 adults, which is 21 people.

**Leaving the children while the adults prepare another room:** No, not unsupervised. All the children are aged 8 to 11, so they are under 12 and "must remain under adult supervision" (hall-safety.md, section 3.3). The rules also require at least one responsible adult to remain near the main entrance (hall-safety.md, section 3.4).

The documents don't say whether an adult at the main entrance counts as supervising the children. They also don't say how close or how many adults supervision requires. So they don't settle whether one adult staying with the children would be enough.

No entry in hall-changes.md replaces any of these sections.
