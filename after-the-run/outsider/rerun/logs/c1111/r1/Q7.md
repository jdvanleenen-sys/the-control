# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "4\\.1|music" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. The music is played on 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April 2026 effective date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies and 21:45 is 15 minutes too late. The change replaces the 22:00 cutoff in hall-conduct.md 4.1 and requires Friday and Saturday amplified music to stop by 21:30 (hall-changes.md, section 5). The controlling date is the date the music is played (hall-changes.md, 5.3).
