# own, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer has an accepted Main Hall booking for Saturday 18 July 2026 and has paid everything due. The booking officer receives their written cancellation on 1 July 2026. What percentage of the room hire charge is refunded, and is the £30 booking deposit returned?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-refunds.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**25% of the room hire charge is refunded, and the £30 booking deposit is not returned.**

- **Percentage:** 1 July to 18 July 2026 is 17 calendar days, which falls in the 14 to 27 day band. The changes register replaces the 50% rate in hall-refunds.md 2.2 with 25%. The controlling date is when the booking officer receives the cancellation (1 July 2026), which is after the 1 June 2026 effective date (hall-changes.md, section 6; hall-refunds.md, sections 1.4 and 2.4).
- **Deposit:** The £30 booking deposit is non-refundable in every hirer cancellation (hall-refunds.md, sections 2.5 and 2.6; hall-changes.md, section 6.4).
