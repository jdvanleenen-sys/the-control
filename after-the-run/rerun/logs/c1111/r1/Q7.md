# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. Amplified music on a Saturday must stop by 21:30, so the band cannot play until 21:45.

The music is played on 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April 2026 effective date. That makes the 21:30 cutoff the controlling rule. It replaces the 22:00 cutoff in the general hall rules (hall-conduct.md, section 4.1). (hall-changes.md, sections 5.1–5.3).
