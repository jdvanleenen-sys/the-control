# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Steps (4)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-bookings.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-safety.md
3. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
4. Grep reference/hall

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people, and that count includes organisers and children (hall-bookings.md, 4.2 and 4.4). Sixteen children plus five adults is 21. No changes-document entry replaces these sections.

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md, 3.3). All the children are aged 8 to 11, so the rule covers them. The documents set no exception for adults preparing another room. No changes-document entry replaces this section.
