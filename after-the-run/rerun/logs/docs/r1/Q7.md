# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (4)

1. Grep Quillbrook
2. Glob **/*{rule,hall,Rule,Hall}*
3. Grep reference/hall
4. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is 15 minutes too late.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, set the Friday and Saturday amplified music cutoff at 22:00. That is the rule the hirer is relying on.
- The committee replaced that section on 20 March 2026 (`hall-changes.md` section 5). From 1 April 2026, Friday and Saturday amplified music must stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played. That date is Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after the 1 April effective date, so the new 21:30 cutoff applies.
- The `hall-quick-guide.md` summary also says 21:30.

The band's contract is a private arrangement and doesn't override the hall rules. The hirer should ask the band to finish by 21:30 or change the contract.
