# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**It doesn't qualify, and the room-hire charge is £48.**

- **Discount:** The group has nine local members, and `hall-fees.md` section 3.2 requires at least ten. Being registered locally isn't enough on its own, so the discount doesn't apply.
- **Rate:** The event is on 15 October 2025. The £28 Main Hall rate in `hall-changes.md` only takes effect for events from 1 November 2025, so the original £24 per hour applies (section 2.1).
- **Charge:** Two paid hours at £24 per hour is £48. Two hours is also the minimum booking (section 2.4).

The £48 covers room hire only. It leaves out the £30 booking deposit, which counts towards the total, and any kitchen or equipment charges. The question doesn't mention those.
