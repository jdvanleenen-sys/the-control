# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. The admission price is the standard £7.**

- **Suspension removes member prices.** Under `film-membership.md` §5.4, "A suspended member cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. Their membership is still active, but `film-fees.md` §2.2 gives the member price only to "members with active membership," and §5.4 overrides that during the suspension.
- **Standard admission is £7.** `film-fees.md` §2.1 sets standard admission at £7 per screening. The age-based rates don't apply to a 35-year-old: under-12s pay £3 and ages 12–15 pay £5.
- **The later increase doesn't matter here.** The committee's 19 January 2026 decision raised the member price from £4 to £5. It applies only to screenings from 1 February 2026, so it wouldn't have affected this screening. The decision also didn't change the standard price.

I didn't read `film-conduct.md` §4, which governs suspensions. The membership rules point to it, but §5.4 already answers the pricing question.
