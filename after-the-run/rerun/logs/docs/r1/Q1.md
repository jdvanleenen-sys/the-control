## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A screening is advertised to begin at 19:30 on 18 November 2025. Someone has paid for a reserved seat but has not arrived when the film starts. Until what exact time must the society hold that seat, and when do the doors normally open?

## Answer

Under the society's screening rules (`reference/film/film-screenings.md`), for the 19:30 screening on 18 November 2025:

- **Seat hold:** The society must hold the reserved seat until **19:40**. Rule 3.4 says reserved seats are held until 10 minutes after the advertised start.
- **Doors:** Doors normally open at **19:00**. Rule 3.3 says doors open 30 minutes before the advertised start.

The committee's changes register (`film-changes.md`) amends the maximum audience and some fee and refund rules. It doesn't touch rules 3.3 or 3.4, so both still apply on that date.

Late arrivals may enter only during a suitable break (rule 3.5). That applies to anyone who turns up after the film has started, including someone whose seat is still being held.
