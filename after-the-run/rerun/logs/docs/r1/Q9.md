# Q9, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: An organiser plans a children's event in the Meeting Room on 19 October 2025. There will be 16 children aged 8 to 11 and five adults, including organisers. The organiser wants to know whether the attendance is permitted and whether the children may be left without adult supervision while the adults prepare another room. What do the rules require?

## Answer

**Attendance: permitted.** The Meeting Room holds a maximum of 24 people (hall-bookings.md 4.2), and that figure includes organisers and children (4.4). Your 16 children and 5 adults make 21, which leaves room for 3 more. The hirer must count attendees when the event begins (hall-safety.md 3.2).

**Leaving the children unsupervised: not permitted.** Children under 12 must remain under adult supervision (hall-safety.md 3.3). Every child aged 8 to 11 is under 12, and the rules have no exception for short periods or for adults preparing another room. So:
- At least one adult must stay with the children the whole time.
- The other adults can go and prepare the other room.
- At least one responsible adult must also stay near the main entrance (hall-safety.md 3.4). The rules don't say whether one adult can do both jobs. If the children are not near the entrance, you would need two adults to stay behind.
- The named hirer must stay on site throughout the event (hall-conduct.md 1.3).

**Other points**
- 19 October 2025 is a Sunday, so the hall closes at 21:00 (hall-bookings.md 3.3), and setup and clearing must fall within the booked hours (3.5).
- If the other room is also hired, the combined limit is 100 people in the building (4.3).
- Cooking equipment is not allowed in hired rooms, and corridors and fire exits must stay clear (hall-safety.md 2.1, 2.6, 3.5). This matters if the other room is being set up with food or equipment.
- None of the committee changes in hall-changes.md affect these rules.

Today is 12 October 2026, so 19 October 2025 has already passed. If you meant 19 October 2026, that is a Monday. Monday to Thursday hours became 08:00 to 21:30 from 1 March 2026, so closing time would differ. The attendance and supervision answers would stay the same.
