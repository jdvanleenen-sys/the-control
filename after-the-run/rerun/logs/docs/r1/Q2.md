# Q2, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the first person pays **£24** and the second person pays **£28**.

- **What decides the fee:** The fee depends on the date the treasurer receives payment (changes register, decision of 16 June 2025, §1.3). Joining date and application date don't matter.
- **First person:** Payment arrived on 30 June 2025, before 1 July 2025. They keep the old fee of £24 (§1.4 of that decision; `film-fees.md` §1.1).
- **Second person:** Payment arrived on 2 July 2025, after the change took effect. Annual adult membership then cost £28 instead of £24 (§1.2 of that decision).

Neither fee is reduced for joining partway through the year (`film-fees.md` §1.3), and there is no joining or administration fee (§1.5).
