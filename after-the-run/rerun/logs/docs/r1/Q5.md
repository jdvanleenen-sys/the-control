> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

The child's ticket costs **£3**.

- **Current rule:** `film-fees.md` §2.3 (effective 1 March 2025) says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. A 9-year-old who is accompanied throughout meets both conditions.
- **The volunteer's £2 price:** This comes from the archived schedule, `film-fees-archive.md` §2.3. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025 and is marked "must not be used for current charges."
- **Later changes:** The committee changes register (`film-changes.md`) has no amendment to the child price. Its only ticket change is the member price (from £4 to £5 on 1 February 2026), which doesn't apply to a child's ticket.

The 16 August 2025 screening date falls after the 1 March 2025 effective date, so the £3 price applies.
