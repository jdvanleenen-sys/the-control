# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Steps (3)

1. Read reference/film/film-fees.md
2. Read reference/film/film-changes.md
3. Grep "suspen" in reference/film

## Answer

No. A suspended member cannot claim member prices, so the member discount doesn't apply on 8 November 2025. They pay standard admission of £7 (film-membership.md, 5.4; film-fees.md, 2.1).

The member price is not in play. The 2026 change to £5 (film-changes.md, section 3) applies only from 1 February 2026, and the £4 price in film-fees.md 2.2 is not what governs here either way. Standard admission in 2.1 is not among the sections the changes document replaces.
