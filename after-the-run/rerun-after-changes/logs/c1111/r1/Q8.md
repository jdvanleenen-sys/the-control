# Q8, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No, it doesn't qualify. A registered village group must have at least ten local members, and this group has nine (hall-fees.md, section 3.2). The group is registered locally, but that doesn't meet the member requirement.

The room-hire charge is £48: two hours at £24 per hour. The event is on 15 October 2025, before the 1 November 2025 effective date of the £28 rate, so the original rate applies (hall-fees.md, section 2.1; hall-changes.md, section 2).
