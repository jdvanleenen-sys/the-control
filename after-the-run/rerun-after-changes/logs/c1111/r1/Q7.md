# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Steps (2)

1. Grep "music|amplified|22:00|21:" in reference/hall
2. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is not allowed.

The committee replaced the 22:00 cutoff (hall-conduct.md, 4.1) with 21:30 for Friday and Saturday amplified music. The change took effect on 1 April 2026 and is decided by the date the music is played (hall-changes.md, section 5). The event is on Saturday 4 April 2026, which is after that date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies.
