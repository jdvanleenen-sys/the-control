# Q10, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: Four trustees want to attend a committee meeting on 15 November 2026, but one proposes joining by video call from home while the other three attend in person. Do the rules allow a remote trustee to count towards the required quorum, and would that make this meeting quorate?

## Steps (2)

1. Read reference/hall/hall-trustees.md
2. Grep "quorum|remote|video|trustee" in reference/hall/hall-changes.md

## Answer

The rules don't say, so I can't give a yes or no on either question. They set the quorum at four trustees (hall-trustees.md, 4.2). They also state that they "do not specify whether trustees may attend remotely" (hall-trustees.md, 6.3). Nothing says whether a trustee on a video call counts towards the quorum. hall-changes.md has nothing on the subject either.

Whether the meeting is quorate on 15 November 2026 turns on whether the remote trustee counts:
- **If they count:** four trustees would be present, which meets the quorum of four.
- **If they don't count:** only three would be present, which falls short.

The documents don't settle which applies.
