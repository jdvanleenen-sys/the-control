# Q6, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A hirer books the Meeting Room on 6 September 2025 and asks for one portable speaker and three sets of folding display boards. Ignoring room hire and deposits, what is the total equipment charge?

## Steps (6)

1. Grep Quillbrook|portable speaker|display board
2. Glob **/*
3. Read reference/hall/hall-fees.md
4. Read reference/hall/hall-quick-guide.md
5. Read reference/hall/hall-changes.md
6. Grep "equipment|speaker|board|6 September|September 2025" in reference/hall

## Answer

The total equipment charge is **£25**.

The current fee schedule (`hall-fees.md`, section 4) applies to this booking. It covers bookings starting on or after 1 April 2025, and 6 September 2025 falls in that period. The charges are:
- Portable speaker: £10 per booking, so £10 for one.
- Folding display boards: £5 per set per booking, so £15 for three sets.

£10 + £15 = **£25**.

The changes register (`hall-changes.md`) doesn't alter any equipment charges. The older £8 and £4 rates in `hall-fees-archive.md` are superseded. The group discount doesn't apply to equipment, so it wouldn't change the total.
