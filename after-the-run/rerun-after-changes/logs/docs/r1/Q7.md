# Q7, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A band is hired for a Saturday evening event on 4 April 2026. Their contract says amplified music will continue until 21:45. The hirer points out that the general hall rules allow Friday and Saturday amplified music until 22:00. May the band play until 21:45?

## Answer

**No. The band must stop by 21:30, so 21:45 is 15 minutes too late.**

- The original rule, `hall-conduct.md` section 4.1, set the Friday and Saturday cutoff at 22:00. That is the rule the hirer is relying on.
- The Committee Changes Register (`hall-changes.md`, section 5) replaced it. The decision was made on 20 March 2026 and took effect on 1 April 2026. Friday and Saturday amplified music must now stop by 21:30.
- The controlling date is the date the music is played (section 5.3), not the booking or contract date. The band plays on 4 April 2026, after the 1 April effective date, so the 21:30 cutoff applies.
- The hall quick guide also says 21:30.

The hirer is using the old wording. The original documents keep their adopted text, but the register overrides section 4.1 for any music played on or after 1 April 2026. The band's contract can't override the hall rule, so the hirer should shorten the set to finish by 21:30.
