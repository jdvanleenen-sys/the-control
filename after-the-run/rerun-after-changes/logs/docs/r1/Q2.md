## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: Two adults have accepted membership applications. The treasurer receives the first person's £24 payment on 30 June 2025 and the second person's payment on 2 July 2025. How much should each person pay for annual adult membership?

4. Read reference/film/film-membership.md

## Answer

Under the Fenwray Film Society rules, the fee depends on the date the treasurer receives each payment:

- **First person, paid 30 June 2025: £24.** This is before 1 July 2025, so the original fee in `film-fees.md` s1.1 applies. The committee's decision of 16 June 2025 says payments received before 1 July 2025 keep the old fee (`film-changes.md` s1.4).
- **Second person, paid 2 July 2025: £28.** From 1 July 2025 the committee raised annual adult membership to £28 (`film-changes.md` s1.2). The payment date decides the fee (s1.3).

Nothing else changes either amount. There is no joining or administration fee (`film-fees.md` s1.5), and the fee isn't reduced for joining partway through the year (s1.3). Payment is only due once an application is accepted (`film-membership.md` s2.5), and that doesn't affect the figures here.
