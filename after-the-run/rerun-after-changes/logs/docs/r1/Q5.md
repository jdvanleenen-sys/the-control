> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A parent brings their 9-year-old child to a public screening on 16 August 2025 and accompanies them throughout. A volunteer has found an old price list showing that children under 12 pay £2. What is the correct ticket price for the child?

## Answer

**The child's ticket costs £3.**

- **Current rule:** Section 2.3 of `film-fees.md` says children under 12 pay £3 when accompanied by an adult. That schedule took effect on 1 March 2025, so it covers a screening on 16 August 2025. The child is 9 and the parent accompanies them throughout, so the condition is met.
- **The old price list:** The volunteer's £2 matches section 2.3 of `film-fees-archive.md`. That schedule applied only from 1 February to 28 February 2025. It was superseded in full on 1 March 2025, and the archive itself says it must not be used for current charges.
- **Later changes:** None of the committee decisions in `film-changes.md` affect child tickets. The only ticket change there is the member price, which went from £4 to £5 from 1 February 2026. That change doesn't apply to the child's ticket.
