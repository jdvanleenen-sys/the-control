# Q4, run 1

## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Fenwray Film Society rules: A member aged 35 has an active annual membership but is suspended by the committee from 1 November through 20 November 2025. They arrive at a public screening on 8 November 2025 and ask to pay the usual member admission price. Are they entitled to the discount, and what admission price applies instead?

## Answer

**No, they aren't entitled to the member discount. They pay £7, the standard admission price.**

- **Suspension:** Their membership is still active, but film-membership.md §5.4 says a suspended member "cannot vote, book equipment or claim member prices." The suspension runs 1–20 November 2025, so 8 November falls inside it. It is also within the 30-day maximum in film-conduct.md §4.1.
- **Price:** Standard admission is £7 per screening (film-fees.md §2.1). A 35-year-old doesn't qualify for the child rates in §2.3 and §2.4.
- **Member price:** The usual member price would have been £4 under film-fees.md §2.2. The change register raises it to £5, but only for screenings from 1 February 2026, so it doesn't apply to this one. It doesn't affect the answer either way.

They can still attend, since being suspended doesn't bar admission. They just pay the standard price. Entry is subject to the capacity rules, which are 42 people for this screening.
