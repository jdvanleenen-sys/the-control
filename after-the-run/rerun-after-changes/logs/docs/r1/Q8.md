## Prompt, exactly as sent

> Under the Quillbrook Village Hall rules: A registered village group has exactly nine local members and supplies that count when booking the Main Hall for two paid hours on 15 October 2025. The organiser requests the village group discount because the group is registered locally. Does it qualify, and what is the room-hire charge without the discount?

## Answer

**It doesn't qualify.** Under hall-fees.md section 3.2, a registered village group needs at least ten local members. This group has nine, so being registered locally isn't enough.

**Room-hire charge without the discount: £48.** That is 2 hours at £24 an hour for the Main Hall, which is also the two-hour minimum (sections 2.1 and 2.4).

The event is on 15 October 2025. The later changes to the Main Hall rate (£28, from 1 November 2025) and to the discount (25%, from 1 February 2026) don't apply, because they take effect after that date. The quick guide shows those newer figures, but it isn't a governing document.

The £48 covers room hire only. A kitchen (£8), equipment, or the £30 booking deposit would be extra. The booking deposit counts toward the total.
